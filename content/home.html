GTA Tax Training

Free, unofficial, source-cited training guides for the taxes administered by the General Tax Administration (GTA) program of the Florida Department of Revenue. Each guide takes a reader from no background to confident, accurate practice, and every fact links back to the official page or document it came from.

Before you start

These guides are study aids. They are not legal advice and do not replace the law, Department rules, or official guidance. When anything here differs from the official source, the official source controls.

How these guides are made

  1. Harvest. Public pages, forms, brochures, Tax Information Publications (TIPs) and tutorial transcripts are downloaded from floridarevenue.com at a slow, polite rate, without signing in, and listed in a manifest with a checksum for each file.
  2. Research. The material is read and organized into a beginner-to-master outline. Rates, thresholds and dates are taken only from those sources, never estimated.
  3. Cite. Every factual statement links to its source. Where sources disagree or may be out of date, the page says so and gives the source's revision date.
  4. Test. Pages are checked with automated accessibility tests (WCAG 2.2 AA), link checks, and fact spot-checks against the sources.
