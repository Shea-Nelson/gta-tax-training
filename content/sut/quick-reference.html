The figures most often needed, each linked to its source. They are current as of the sources retrieved in November 2026. Check the TIP index for later changes. {{cite:tips-page}} This page prints on its own.

Rates

  • General state rate: 6% {{cite:sut-page}}
  • New mobile homes: 3% (since July 1, 2022) {{cite:tip-22a01-12}}
  • Amusement machine receipts: 4% {{cite:gt800020}}
  • Electricity: 6.95% {{cite:gt300015}}
  • Commercial rent: repealed for periods on or after October 1, 2025; 2% for earlier periods {{cite:tip-25a01-04}}
  • County surtax: see the DR-15DSS for the current year {{cite:dr15dss_26}}

Surtax rules

  • Goods: county where delivered {{cite:gt300015}}
  • Services: county where performed {{cite:gt300015}}
  • Vehicles, mobile homes, boat-and-trailer combos: buyer's county on the title or registration {{cite:sut-page}} {{cite:tip-24a01-11}}
  • $5,000 limit: per single item of tangible personal property only, not admissions, services, warranties, transient rentals or parking {{cite:gt300015}}

Rounding

  • Carry tax to the third decimal and round up if the third decimal is greater than 4 (since July 1, 2021) {{cite:tip-21a01-02}}
  • Rounding cash totals to the nearest nickel: see TIP 26A01-03 {{cite:tip-26a01-03}}

Filing

  • Due the 1st; late after the 20th of the following month {{cite:dr15n}}
  • E-payment: start it and get a confirmation number by 5 p.m. ET on the business day before the 20th {{cite:dr15n}}
  • Frequency by annual tax: over $1,000 monthly; $501–$1,000 quarterly; $101–$500 semiannual; $100 or less annual {{cite:sut-page}} {{cite:gt300015}}
  • E-filing required if $5,000 or more was paid in the prior state fiscal year {{cite:sut-page}}
  • Estimated tax if $200,000 or more was paid in the prior state fiscal year {{cite:dr15n}}
  • File a return for every period, even when no tax is due {{cite:dr15n}}
  • Final return within 15 days of closing {{cite:gt300015}}

Allowance, penalty, interest

  • Collection allowance: 2.5% of the first $1,200 of Line 10, maximum $30, electronic and on time only {{cite:dr15n}}
  • Late penalty: 10% of Line 10, minimum $50 {{cite:dr15n}}
  • E-file or e-pay failure: $10 each {{cite:sut-page}}
  • Interest: 11% (daily factor 0.000301370) from January 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 {{cite:tip-26adm-02}} {{cite:tip-26adm-06}}

Certificates

  • Annual Resale Certificate (DR-13): expires December 31 {{cite:sut-page}}
  • Consumer's Certificate of Exemption (DR-14): valid five years {{cite:dr5}}
  • Verification line: (877) 357-3725 {{cite:tip-24a01-01}}
  • Keep copies of certificates for three years {{cite:arc-page}}

Thresholds and limits

  • Remote sellers: over $100,000 in taxable remote sales in the prior calendar year {{cite:tip-21a01-03}}
  • Transient rental: six months or less {{cite:sut-page}}
  • Records: at least three years {{cite:gt300015}}
  • Refund claims: within three years of payment {{cite:refunds-page}}
  • Amusement machine certificate: $30 per machine per year, expires June 30 {{cite:gt800020}}

Audit deadlines

  • DR-840 notice period: 60 days {{cite:gt800022}}
  • DR-1215 response: 30 days {{cite:gt800022}}
  • Protest of a Notice of Proposed Assessment: 60 days informal, 120 days formal {{cite:gt800022}}
