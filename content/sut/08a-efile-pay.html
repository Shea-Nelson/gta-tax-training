Learning objectives

  • Explain what changed when the Department moved sales and use tax to the new eFile and Pay system on December 1, 2025.
  • Decide whether a business must or simply may file and pay electronically.
  • Log in as an enrolled user or a guest user, file a DR-15 and pay it, following the Department's own step-by-step guides.
  • Apply the electronic payment deadline in Form DR-659 and keep the confirmation number.
  • Know where consolidated filers, bulk filers and software users fit, and where to send someone who is stuck.

This page is a companion to Module 8: Filing and paying. Module 8 explains what goes on each line of the return. This page explains how to get that return, and the money, to the Department electronically. Every screen name and button label below is quoted from a Department guide or webpage. Where the sources do not describe a step, this page says so rather than guessing.

Screens change

The Department revises its guides as the system changes. The DR-15 guide used here is dated October 2026. If what a taxpayer sees differs from this page, the current guide on the eFile and Pay Information Center wins. {{cite:dr15-electronicfilepayguide,filepayinfo-resources}}

What the new system is, and what changed on December 1, 2025

The Department replaced its electronic file and pay website in phases, with a new vendor hosting the new system. It is free and secure, and it has a modernized design with help messages on most screens. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}} The first release, on April 1, 2025, covered gross receipts tax on utility services and use tax on out-of-state purchases. {{cite:filepayinfo-dash}} The second release moved sales and use tax, solid waste fees and surcharges, and the prepaid wireless fee onto the new system on December 1, 2025, along with printing the sales and use tax Annual Resale Certificate. {{cite:tip-25a01-16,filepayinfo-dash}}

What carried over, and what did not:

  • Login details carried over. Taxpayers use their existing User ID and Password, or their Certificate Number and Business Partner Number. {{cite:tip-25a01-16}} Enrolled users' profiles transferred. Guest users' account numbers transferred, but profile details such as bank accounts are not visible. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}}
  • Saved logins did not. Credentials saved in the old system, or by a browser for the old site, did not transfer. The Department told taxpayers to find their login information and store it somewhere secure. {{cite:tip-25a01-16,tip-25a01-19}}
  • Filing history did not. Prior returns and confirmation numbers were not converted to the new system. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}} Copies can be requested at any time with Form DR-841, Request for Copy of Tax Return. {{cite:sutlogin-page,dr841}}
  • Due dates did not change, and neither did the rules on who must file and pay electronically. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}}
  • Old bookmarks do not work. The old site's buttons are grayed out, and it can't be used to file or pay. {{cite:tip-25a01-19}}

Conflicting dates for the old system's history

The sources disagree about how long the old site stayed open for downloading history. A November 3, 2025 notice said March 31, 2026. {{cite:filepayinfo-dash}} The December 1, 2025 notice on the sales and use tax login page says September 30, 2026. {{cite:sutlogin-page}} The FAQ says “one to two months” after each tax moved. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}} Every one of those dates has passed, so today the answer is the same: request copies with Form DR-841. {{cite:dr841}}

Communications services tax moved to the new eFile and Pay system on its own date and has its own login page and guide. See CST Module 13.

Who must file electronically, and who may

  • Anyone may. Any business can file and pay electronically, through the Department's website or with software bought from a vendor. {{cite:dr15n}} A business not required to file electronically is not required to now just because the system changed. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}}
  • Required: $5,000 or more. A business that paid $5,000 or more in sales and use tax during the state's prior fiscal year (July 1 to June 30) must file and pay electronically during the next calendar year. {{cite:howtocompletedr15,sut-page}}
  • Required: consolidated filers. A business that files a consolidated return must also file and pay electronically. {{cite:gt800002}}
  • Penalty. A business that is required to file and pay electronically but does not faces a $10 penalty for not filing electronically and another $10 for not paying electronically, on top of any other penalty. {{cite:sut-page}}
  • Waiver. A business that cannot file electronically can ask for a waiver on Form DR-654, Request for Waiver from Electronic Filing. The form says an approved waiver may last up to two years, and that the business must still pay electronically. {{cite:dr654}} The forms index lists the DR-654 as informational and says to call 850-488-6800 to request one. {{cite:forms-page}}

Enrolled users, guest users, and enrolling

The new system uses two terms: {{cite:tip-25a01-19}}

  • Enrolled User. A taxpayer who enrolled for eServices and was issued a User ID and Password. Log in with the “Log In” button under the Enrolled User section.
  • Guest User. A taxpayer who is not enrolled, or who chooses to log in with the Certificate Number and Business Partner Number. Do not include leading zeros in the Business Partner Number.

Enrollment is not required to file sales and use tax. {{cite:enroll-page}} Taxpayers can also use other credentials, such as the Certificate Number, Business Partner Number or Federal Employer Identification Number. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}} The Department does encourage enrollment, because enrolled taxpayers can save their bank account and contact information, view filing history, reprint returns and view bills posted to the account. {{cite:enroll-page}} Enrolled users can also see and cancel scheduled payments, including ones submitted as a guest. A guest who needs to cancel a scheduled payment must call Taxpayer Services. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}}

How to enroll. Enroll online through the Department's Enroll to eFile and Pay page; the online application covers every tax at once. On paper, Form DR-600, Enrollment and Authorization for eServices, takes one form per tax. {{cite:enroll-page,dr600}} Two side effects: enrolled taxpayers stop receiving payment coupons in the mail, and enrolling does not sign anyone up for due date reminders, which need a separate subscription. {{cite:enroll-page}}

What to have on hand

  • Login details: a User ID and Password, or a Certificate Number and Business Partner Number. {{cite:tip-25a01-19}} The certificate number is printed on the Annual Resale Certificate and the Certificate of Registration. {{cite:tip-25a01-16}} The Business Partner Number appears on billing notices, delinquency notices and resale certificates. {{cite:passwordretrievalguide}}
  • The period's figures for each line of the return, including surtax by county for Lines 15(a) to 15(d). {{cite:dr15-electronicfilepayguide}} Module 8 covers what goes on each line.
  • Bank details for an ACH debit: bank name, ABA routing number, account number and account type. Payments funded from banks outside the US or its territories cannot be processed as ACH transactions. {{cite:dr600}}
  • The company ID, if the account has a debit block. A business whose bank blocks unauthorized debits must give the bank the Department's company identifier. For sales and use tax it is 7596001874. Without it, the bank may reject the payment, and the Department may bill the amount due with penalty and interest. {{cite:companyidentifiers,enroll-page}}
  • A previous confirmation number from an earlier electronic filing, in case the password needs resetting. {{cite:tip-25a01-19,passwordretrievalguide}}

The process at a glance

Filing a DR-15, step by step

These steps follow the Department's Sales and Use Tax Return (Form DR-15) Electronic File and Pay Step-by-Step Guide, dated October 2026. Labels in quotation marks are the guide's own words. {{cite:dr15-electronicfilepayguide}}

  1. Navigate. From the Department's home page, click the File and Pay icon. Under the eFile and Pay Taxes and Fees section, select the Sales and Use Tax link.
  2. Log in. Click “Log In” and enter the User ID and Password, then “Sign in.” To use a Certificate Number and Business Partner Number instead, the guide says to click “Start a New Return.” A Privacy Notice appears next; click OK to continue.
  3. Start the return. Click ONLINE TRANSACTIONS and select “File a Tax Return/Report.” Fill in the Return Information, including the Form Type (DR-15), and click Next.
  4. Return Header tab. Review the taxpayer and return information. The “Next Tab” button moves through the tabs.
  5. DR-15 Sales Details tab. Enter the sales information.
  6. DR-15 tab. Enter the return figures. On Line 11, a checkbox lets the dealer donate the collection allowance to education. Enter surtax on Lines 15(a) to 15(d), click Calculate, then Next Tab.
  7. Estimated Tax Worksheet tab. Choose one calculation method. Method 1 averages prior filings in the calendar year; Method 2 uses the same month of the previous year; Method 3 lets the filer enter an amount. Module 8 explains who owes estimated tax.
  8. Vending and Amusement Worksheet (under the More tab). Fill in any vending or amusement fields, click Calculate, then Continue. Continue stays unavailable until Calculate has been clicked. “Save and Exit” lets the filer finish later.
  9. Summary Information. Review and click Next.
  10. Signature. Complete the required fields and click Yes to authorize submission.
  11. Confirmation. A green Confirmation banner appears. Print a copy, then select “Make a Payment Now,” or “Return Home” to pay later under ONLINE TRANSACTIONS, Make a Payment.

The DR-15EZ has its own step-by-step guide with the same structure. {{cite:dr15ez-electronicfilepayguide}} Other points from the Department's FAQ: {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}}

  • An unsubmitted return is saved for up to 90 days.
  • The system times out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
  • After submission, the confirmation appears in the Message Center, and the return's status appears in filing history. Inbox messages are kept indefinitely; they cover payment confirmations, canceled ACH payments, and returns filed, saved or canceled.
  • A submitted return can be canceled if a trash can icon is shown for it; its status then changes to Canceled.

Two names for the guest button

TIP 25A01-19 (December 2025) tells guest users to select “File a Return” under the Guest User section. {{cite:tip-25a01-19}} The October 2026 DR-15 guide and the sales and use tax login page call it “Start a New Return.” {{cite:dr15-electronicfilepayguide,sutlogin-page}} Teach the newer label and mention the older one.

Returns with no tax due

A return is required for every reporting period, even if no tax is due. {{cite:dr15n}} The Department's tutorial adds: don't skip periods or fold a partial period into the next return. {{cite:howtocompletedr15}} Dealers with nothing due who claim no deductions or credits can telefile at 800-550-6713, have their certificate number ready, and keep the confirmation number. A dealer who telefiles must not mail the paper return. {{cite:dr15n}}

Gap: none of the Department's public eFile guides walks through a zero return on screen. Use the regular DR-15 or DR-15EZ steps, enter the figures, and keep the confirmation.

Paying right after filing

From “Make a Payment Now”: {{cite:dr15-electronicfilepayguide}}

  1. Enter the Payment Amount, or check the box to pay the Total Outstanding Balance, and select the Payment Method.
  2. Review the contact information and click Next.
  3. On Schedule Electronic Payment, choose a saved bank account or enter a new one.
  4. Review the Debit Date and change it if needed. Check the authorization box and click Next.
  5. On Confirm Payment, check the details and click Confirm (or Back to change something).
  6. A green Confirmation banner appears. Print it, then Log Out.

Paying without filing: the payment-only website

For a bill, or a payment made separately from the return: {{cite:efilepayguide-paymentonlywebsite,paymentonly-page}}

  1. Click the File and Pay icon, then, under “ePay a Bill or Make a Payment,” select “Tax/Fee/Remittance Payment Only.”
  2. Choose “Log In with User ID and Password,” or “Make a Payment Without Logging In” to use other credentials such as the Business Partner Number, Certificate Number and FEIN.
  3. Under ONLINE TRANSACTIONS, select Make a Payment. Choose the Type of Payment, complete the required fields, including the Filing Period End Date, and enter the amount, payment method and contact details.
  4. For ACH debit, enter the bank details or use an account on file. Choose a Debit Date, check the authorization box, click Next, then Confirm. Keep the confirmation.

The system does not show outstanding balances, so “current balance” reads $0.00 even when a bill is owed. To pay a bill, select the filing period under Online Transactions and type in the amount. Alternatively, use the Online Tax Bill Payment system with the OCR number printed on the notice. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}}

Deadlines, scheduling and confirmation numbers

Returns and payments are due on the 1st and late after the 20th. {{cite:sut-page}} An electronic payment takes one business day to clear the banking system. To be on time, the payment must be completed, and its confirmation number received, by 5 p.m. ET on the date in the Department's Calendar of Electronic Payment Deadlines, Form DR-659. {{cite:dr659}} The other rules on the form: {{cite:dr659}}

  • The debit date cannot be a weekend or a state or federal holiday. The earliest date that can be selected is the next business day after the payment is started.
  • A payment started on the deadline day cannot carry that day's debit date. It is still on time if the confirmation number arrives before 5 p.m. ET.
  • A payment completed after 5 p.m. ET on the deadline is processed the next business day. That makes it late, and penalty and interest may apply.
  • Keep the confirmation number. Software payments generally follow the same calendar, but vendors may set their own deadlines.

Canceling a payment

Only an ACH debit can be canceled, and only before 5 p.m. ET on the day it was submitted (or the next business day after a weekend or holiday submission). Card payments cannot be canceled. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}} The Department's cancel guide adds these steps: {{cite:efilepayguide-cancelpayment}}

  1. Log in with a User ID and Password; guests cannot cancel online.
  2. Under ONLINE TRANSACTIONS, select Submitted Items. In Transaction History, click the garbage can icon in the Action column. If there is no icon, it is too late to cancel.
  3. Confirm by clicking Cancel Payment, then OK. The status changes to Transaction Canceled.

The collection allowance connection

A dealer can take the collection allowance only when the return is filed and the tax paid electronically, both on time. It is 2.5% of the first $1,200 of tax due, so no more than $30, and it goes on Line 11. {{cite:dr15n}} The electronic form puts a checkbox on Line 11 for donating the allowance to education instead. {{cite:dr15-electronicfilepayguide}} If the confirmation number arrives after the DR-659 deadline, the payment is late and the allowance is lost. {{cite:dr659,dr15n}}

Amending electronically

An amended return replaces the original for the same period, so it is completed in full, the way the original should have been filed. The quickest way to amend is online. {{cite:dr15n}}

Gap: no public Department guide shows the screens for amending in the new system. Don't invent the steps. Refer taxpayers to the current guides, or to Taxpayer Services at 850-488-6800. {{cite:filepayinfo-resources,tip-25a01-19}}

Multiple locations: consolidated returns

A business with several locations that files one consolidated return must file and pay electronically. {{cite:gt800002}} The consolidated return guide works like this: {{cite:dr7dr15con-electronicfilepayguide}}

  1. In File a Tax Return/Report, choose Form Type DR-15CON. The Consolidated Return screen lists every location that can be filed together.
  2. For each Location ID, click Open in the Return Status column to launch a DR-7. Complete the DR-7 Sales Details and DR-7 tabs, including surtax Lines 15(a) to 15(d), the Estimated Tax and Vending and Amusement worksheets, then Save. A finished location shows Saved; one left with Save and Exit shows In Progress.
  3. Once every location shows Saved, click Review Consolidated Return. The DR-15CON tabs total the DR-7s. Click Calculate, Continue and Next, sign, and submit.
  4. Pay the same way as for a single return.

Software, bulk filing and Direct File

  • Vendor software. Sales and use tax returns can also be filed through approved software. {{cite:gt800002}} The Department publishes a list of software vendors for eFiling in XML format, including some for sales and use tax, and states it gives no preference to any company or product. {{cite:fl_efilevendor}} Vendors may set their own payment deadlines, so check with them. {{cite:dr659}}
  • Direct File (formerly SecureNet). Filers who upload return files register a new transmitter account. The User ID must differ from their File and Pay User ID. They receive a temporary password by email, set three security questions and a new password, and the account becomes fully active about 30 minutes later. Then they go to TRANSMITTER TRANSACTIONS, File Bulk Returns, pick a File Type (for example Production-Sales Tax or Test-Sales Tax), and submit one file at a time. A red banner explains any failure. View Transmissions shows the manifest for each upload. {{cite:efilepayguide-directfile}}
  • Password and files. Direct filers' new passwords must be at least 14 characters. {{cite:filepayinfo-faq}} Portal uploads must be .xml or .zip. SFTP is required for bulk uploads, and the portal takes single files only. {{cite:filepayinfo-dash}} Files over 75 MB must be zipped, and plain FTP is no longer supported. {{cite:filepayinfo-dash}} A test file submitted as production, or the reverse, is rejected with an error code. {{cite:efilepayguide-securefiletransmission}}
  • Developers who want to offer electronic filing should email e-vendor@floridarevenue.com about testing and approval. {{cite:vendor-page}}

The older Secure File Transmission (Bulk Filer) guide (November 2025) describes the same registration under earlier screen names. The October 2026 Direct File guide is the current one. {{cite:efilepayguide-securefiletransmission,efilepayguide-directfile}}

Quarterly, semiannual and annual filers

These filers had no December 2025 return, so their first use of the new system came in January 2026. TIP 25A01-19 was written for them. {{cite:tip-25a01-19}} Its points still apply:

  • Go to floridarevenue.com/taxes/filepay and select Sales and Use Tax under eFile and Pay Taxes and Fees.
  • Use the same login details as before. Saved credentials did not move over.
  • The Department strongly encouraged filing and paying early, to get used to the new system and avoid waits for help.
  • The DR-659 deadline applies to their due month exactly as it does for monthly filers. {{cite:dr659}}

Coquina Candle Co. files and pays July 2026

Fictional business, county with no surtax, all figures invented. Coquina Candle Co. paid $6,200 in sales and use tax in the state fiscal year ending June 30, 2025, so it must file and pay electronically in calendar 2026. It is a monthly filer, enrolled for eServices, and its bank has a debit block.

  1. Before August. The owner gives the bank the sales and use tax company ID, 7596001874.
  2. Deadline. The July return is due August 1 and late after August 20. DR-659 lists Wednesday, August 19, 2026 as the deadline: the confirmation number must arrive by 5 p.m. ET that day.
  3. Tuesday, August 18, 2 p.m. ET. The owner logs in with the User ID and Password, files the DR-15 and reaches Line 10: $1,050.00 tax due.
  4. Collection allowance. Filing and paying electronically and on time, the business takes 2.5% × $1,050.00 = $26.25 on Line 11. That is under the $30 cap, and the business keeps it rather than checking the donation box.
  5. Payment. Amount due: $1,050.00 − $26.25 = $1,023.75. Make a Payment Now, ACH debit, saved bank account. The earliest debit date offered is the next business day, Wednesday, August 19. Confirm. A green banner shows the confirmation number, which the owner prints.
  6. A slip-up. At 3 p.m. the owner notices the wrong account was chosen. It is before 5 p.m. ET on the day of submission and the owner is logged in, so the payment can be canceled under Submitted Items and remade straight away. The new confirmation still arrives well before the deadline.

What if? If the owner had waited until 5:15 p.m. ET on August 19, the payment would have been processed on August 20. It would be late, the $26.25 allowance would be lost, and penalty and interest could apply (Module 17).

Key takeaways

  • Since December 1, 2025, sales and use tax is filed and paid at floridarevenue.com/taxes/filepay in the new system. Old bookmarks and saved logins don't work.
  • Electronic filing is required after a state fiscal year with $5,000 or more paid, and for consolidated filers. Anyone else may choose it.
  • Enrolled users log in with User ID and Password; guests use Certificate Number and Business Partner Number, with no leading zeros.
  • Payment confirmation by 5 p.m. ET on the DR-659 date. Keep every confirmation number.
  • An ACH debit can be canceled until 5 p.m. ET on the day it was submitted; a card payment can't be canceled.
