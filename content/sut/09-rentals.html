Learning objectives

  • Identify a taxable transient rental and the local taxes that come with it.
  • Apply the October 1, 2025 repeal of the commercial rent tax, including payments for earlier periods.
  • Report parking, docking, tie-down and storage, which the repeal did not change.
  • Recognize when rentals of tangible personal property and vehicle leases are taxable.

Transient rentals

Rentals or leases of living, sleeping or housekeeping accommodations for six months or less are taxable. These are accommodations in hotels, motels, apartments, rooming houses, mobile home parks, RV parks, condominiums or timeshare resorts. Counties may add local option transient rental taxes, such as tourist development taxes. {{cite:sut-page}} {{cite:tip-25a01-04}}

  • State sales tax and surtax on transient rentals are always reported to the Department. Many counties collect their local transient rental taxes directly. {{cite:sut-page}} {{cite:gt300015}}
  • Form DR-15TDT lists local option transient rental tax rates for every county, including whether each is paid to the Department or to the county. {{cite:gt300015}} {{cite:dr15tdt}}
  • Surtax is sourced to the county where the accommodation is located, and the $5,000 limit does not apply. {{cite:gt300015}}
  • Owners of several rental units can register them together using Form DR-1C, Application for Collective Registration of Living or Sleeping Accommodations. {{cite:dr1c}}
  • Businesses that rent transient accommodations cannot use the DR-15EZ. {{cite:dr15ezn}}

Commercial rentals: repealed October 1, 2025

State sales tax and surtax no longer apply to rent or license fees for commercial real property for rental or occupancy periods beginning on or after October 1, 2025. Examples include office or retail space, warehouses and self-storage units. {{cite:tip-25a01-04}}

Timing traps (TIP 25A01-04, issued July 24, 2025)

  • Rent for periods through September 2025 is still taxable at the 2% state rate plus surtax, even if paid on or after October 1, 2025. Paying late does not avoid tax.
  • Rent paid in September 2025 for October 2025 or later is not taxable.
  • Non-taxable rent should not be reported on the return.
  • Accounts used only for commercial rentals must file through the final period (for example, the July–December 2025 semiannual return) even if no tax is due.

Outdated source

TIP 24A01-16R (revised November 12, 2024) says salons that license booth space to independent stylists owe 2% tax on the license fee. That TIP predates the repeal. For periods beginning October 1, 2025 or later, follow TIP 25A01-04. {{cite:tip-24a01-16}} {{cite:tip-25a01-04}}

Parking, docking, tie-down and storage: still taxable

The 6% state tax plus surtax still applies to: {{cite:tip-25a01-11}}

  • parking or storage spaces for motor vehicles in lots or garages, including storage for towed vehicles;
  • docking or storage spaces for boats in docks or marinas;
  • tie-down or storage space for aircraft at airports.

These were never commercial rentals under the repealed section, so the repeal does not affect them. Charges from lawful impoundment by law enforcement are not taxable. On the DR-15, report these charges on Line A, not Line C or Line D. {{cite:tip-25a01-11}}

Department example

Mr. Smith operates a downtown parking lot on land he leases from Mr. Jones. The $5 per hour and $25 per day fees his customers pay are taxable at 6% plus surtax. Mr. Smith's lease payment to Mr. Jones is not taxable. {{cite:tip-25a01-11}}

Renting tangible personal property

Rentals or leases of tangible personal property, such as tables, display cases or cash registers used at a concession stand, are taxable at 6% plus the surtax of the county where the item is used. {{cite:gt800003}}

Long-term business vehicle leases

Since July 1, 2024, a lease or rental of a motor vehicle for use in the lessee's trade or business is not taxable when: {{cite:tip-24a01-08}}

  • the lessor paid sales or use tax on the vehicle's purchase price; and
  • the lease is for 12 months or more.

The exclusion also covers renewals. {{cite:tip-24a01-08}}

Gulf Breeze Rentals (fictional)

  • Gulf Breeze rents a beach condo for a two-week stay: taxable transient rental. It reports state tax and surtax to the Department, and pays the county's tourist development tax to whichever agency DR-15TDT shows.
  • It also rents a small office to an accounting firm. Rent for October 2025 onward is not taxable, but the firm's late payment of September 2025 rent is taxable at 2% plus surtax.
  • Boat slips it rents at its dock stay taxable and go on Line A.

Rules applied: {{cite:sut-page}} {{cite:tip-25a01-04}} {{cite:tip-25a01-11}}

Key takeaways

  • Transient rentals of six months or less are taxable, plus surtax and any local transient rental tax.
  • Commercial rent tax is repealed for periods beginning on or after October 1, 2025. Earlier periods stay taxable whenever paid.
  • Parking, docking, tie-down and storage remain taxable at 6% and are reported on Line A.
