Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

  • Give the effective date and notice deadlines for a normal local rate change.
  • Explain how surtax changes and court rulings change local rates.
  • Apply the freeze on local rate increases through December 31, 2030.
  • Keep a billing system current with the Department's notices.

The normal schedule

Any adoption, repeal or change of a local CST rate takes effect for services on bills dated on or after the January 1 after the change. The city or county must notify the Department by September 1 before that January 1, on the Department's form, which is DR-700021, giving the rate, the effective date and a contact person. The Department must notify affected dealers at least 90 days before the effective date. {{cite:s202-21,r12a19-050,dr700021}}

The bill date controls, not the service date. The Department's January 2026 notice said its new rates applied “for all bills issued on or after January 1, 2026, regardless of the date the service is rendered.” {{cite:tip-25a19-02}} A local government that adjusts its rate by emergency ordinance under s. 202.20(2) must notify the Department immediately, and the Department must notify affected dealers within 30 days. {{cite:s202-21}}

The freeze through 2030

A local CST rate in effect on January 1, 2023 may not be increased before January 1, 2031, and any increase to a county discretionary sales surtax on or after January 1, 2023 may not be added to the local CST before then. {{cite:s202-19,tip-25a19-01}} The 2025 Legislature extended a freeze originally announced in TIP 23A19-01, which had been set to end December 31, 2025. {{cite:tip-25a19-01,tip-23a19-01}} Rates can still go down: the January 2026 changes were all decreases. {{cite:tip-25a19-02}}

Changes driven by the county surtax

Because the total local rate includes the county surtax conversion rate (Module 3), a change in a county's surtax changes the CST rate of every jurisdiction in that county. {{cite:tip-25a19-02,s202-20}}

Recent changes

  • January 1, 2026, Martin and Palm Beach counties: rates in jurisdictions in both counties changed because of a change in the county surtax. Stuart, for example, went to 5.220%. {{cite:tip-25a19-02,cst-rate-table}}
  • March 2021, Hillsborough County: after the Florida Supreme Court struck down the county's 1% transportation surtax, the Department revised the local CST rates effective immediately, for example Tampa 6.020% and Plant City 6.520%. {{cite:tip-21a19-01}}
  • January 1, 2023: the City of Lake Worth was renamed Lake Worth Beach on the return with no rate change, and Bonita Springs changed its rate's expiration date. {{cite:tip-22a19-02}}

Jurisdictions added and removed

Jurisdictions also come and go: TIPs have announced new taxing jurisdictions and removed others, such as the Town of Hastings and the City of Weeki Wachee. Dealers must add and remove them in their systems on the dates given. {{cite:tip-18a19-01,tip-18a19-02,tip-20a19-01}}

Address changes have their own calendar

Changes to which addresses belong to which jurisdiction are separate from rate changes. They take effect only on January 1 or July 1, after local governments submit them at least 120 days ahead (Module 8). {{cite:dr700002}}

Staying current: a checklist

  1. Subscribe to the Department's TIP email alerts and read every TIP numbered with “A19,” the communications services tax series. {{cite:gt800011,tips-page}}
  2. Each fall, download the current “Historical, Current and Upcoming Local Rates” workbook and look for upcoming rates in red bold. {{cite:cst-rate-table,gt800011}}
  3. Each January and July, download the updated address database. {{cite:gt800011,pointmatch-files}}
  4. Bill using the rate in effect on the bill date.

Rates for January 1, 2027

When this guide's sources were collected in November 2026, the Department's TIP index had no 2026 communications services tax TIPs, and the rate workbook (dated January 2026) showed no upcoming rates. Both were unchanged when checked again on November 22, 2026. Any January 1, 2027 changes would normally be announced in late November or December. Check the TIP index and rate table for them. {{cite:tips-page,cst-rate-table}}

Key takeaways

  • Normal changes: local notice by September 1, Department notice at least 90 days ahead, effective for bills dated on or after January 1.
  • No increases before January 1, 2031; decreases are allowed.
  • Surtax changes and court rulings can change CST rates.
  • Address assignments change only January 1 and July 1.
