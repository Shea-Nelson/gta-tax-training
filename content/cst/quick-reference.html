Rates

  • State CST 4.92% + gross receipts 0.15% = 5.07% “state tax” {{cite:cst-page}}
  • Gross receipts 2.37%; Florida CST total 7.44% {{cite:cst-page}}
  • Direct-to-home satellite: 9.07% + 2.37% = 11.44%, no local tax {{cite:cst-page}}
  • Residential landline or VoIP: 2.37% + local (not mobile, video, satellite or transient lodging) {{cite:s202-125}}
  • Local: adopted rate + surtax conversion rate, by jurisdiction; 0.30% to 7.70% in January 2026 {{cite:cst-rate-table}}

Rounding

  • Carry to three decimals; round up when the third decimal is 5 or more {{cite:tip-11a19-01}}
  • Round local tax separately from Florida CST {{cite:tip-11a19-01}}
  • Per item allowed if the total is at least the invoice-level result {{cite:tip-11a19-01}}

Deadlines

  • Return and payment due the 1st, late after the 20th; file even with no tax due {{cite:cst-page}}
  • Electronic payment: confirmation number by 5:00 p.m. ET the business day before the 20th (DR-659) {{cite:cst-page,dr659}}
  • Mandatory electronic filing: $5,000 or more paid in the prior July–June fiscal year {{cite:cst-page}}
  • DR-700019 use tax: periods ending June 30 and December 31, late after July 20 and January 20 {{cite:dr700019}}
  • Local rate change: notice by September 1; effective for bills dated on or after January 1; no increases before January 1, 2031 {{cite:s202-21,s202-19}}
  • Address database changes: January 1 and July 1 {{cite:dr700002}}

Allowance and penalties

  • Collection allowance 0.75% (qualifying address method, or satellite) or 0.25% {{cite:gt800011}}
  • Late: 10% per 30 days or fraction, up to 50% {{cite:cst-page}}
  • Interest July–December 2026: 11% a year, daily factor 0.000301370 {{cite:tip-26adm-02}}
  • No Schedule I: $5,000 per return. Wrong jurisdiction without a protected method: 10%, up to $10,000 per return {{cite:s202-28}}

Resale and exemptions

  • CST resale certificate DR-700015, expires December 31 {{cite:cst-page}}
  • Verify online or at 877-357-3725; batch up to 50,000 accounts {{cite:gt800011}}
  • Exempt: resale, Internet access, governments (direct pay), 501(c)(3) religious and educational institutions, qualifying homes for the aged {{cite:cst-page}}
  • Direct pay permit: DR-700030; five years; renew on DR-700032 {{cite:r12a19-030}}
