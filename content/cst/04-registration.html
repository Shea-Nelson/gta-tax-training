Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

  • Decide whether a business must register as a communications services dealer.
  • Name the forms and documents involved in registering.
  • Explain the address-assignment notice every collecting dealer must give.
  • Know when an account change is enough and when a new registration is needed.

Who must register

A business that sells communications services, such as telephone, video, direct-to-home satellite or related services, must register as a communications services dealer before doing business. {{cite:gt800011,s202-17,r12a19-010}} One registration covers each legal entity, however many locations it has. {{cite:gt800011}} Registration is online through the Department's registration page, or on paper with Form DR-1, Florida Business Tax Application. {{cite:cst-page,dr1,registration-page}}

Failing or refusing to register when required is a first-degree misdemeanor, and a business that fails or refuses to register must pay an initial registration fee of $100, which the Department may waive for reasonable cause. {{cite:s202-17}}

Who does not need to register

Businesses whose only sales fall outside CST do not register, because they have nothing to collect. Rule 12A-19.010 and the Department's guidance give examples such as sellers of information services, Internet access, customer premises wiring or equipment, advertising, billing and collection services, coin-operated telephone calls, prepaid calling arrangements, and faxes sent as part of professional services. {{cite:r12a19-010,dr700016n}}

Pay telephone operators are a good illustration. Charges at a coin-operated phone are not subject to CST, so an operator need not register, but it may choose to register so it can buy the phone lines it resells tax-free with a resale certificate. {{cite:faq-1281}}

What a new dealer receives

After registering, a new dealer receives a Certificate of Registration (DR-700014) and an Annual Resale Certificate for Communications Services Tax (DR-700015). {{cite:cst-page,tip-07a19-05}} The law requires the Department to give each new dealer an initial resale certificate for the rest of the year and each active dealer an annual one. {{cite:s202-17}} The CST resale certificate is separate from the sales and use tax resale certificate. Module 10 explains how it is used.

Telling the Department how you assign addresses

A dealer that collects local CST must tell the Department which method it uses to assign customer addresses to the correct taxing jurisdiction. The method is chosen on the registration application, and a later change is reported on Form DR-700020, Notification of Method Employed to Determine Taxing Jurisdiction. {{cite:gt800011,dr700020,s202-22}} The method decides the dealer's collection allowance (0.75% or 0.25%), so this choice matters; Module 8 covers it. {{cite:gt800011}}

Keeping the account current

Changes such as a new business name, a new mailing or location address, or closing the account can be made online. A change in legal entity or ownership requires a new registration. {{cite:cst-page,faq-1319}}

Example: who registers?

Fictional businesses. {{cite:gt800011,r12a19-010,faq-1281}}

  • Pelican Fiber LLC sells home Internet and a VoIP phone line. It must register, because VoIP is a taxable communications service, even though its Internet access charges are not taxed.
  • Harbor Web Hosting only hosts websites. Web hosting is an information service, so it does not register for CST.
  • Coquina Payphones runs coin telephones. It need not register, but may register to buy its lines for resale.
  • Pelican Fiber later merges into a new corporation. The new legal entity must file a new registration rather than update the old account.

Key takeaways

  • Register before selling taxable communications services; one registration per legal entity.
  • New dealers get DR-700014 and the CST Annual Resale Certificate, DR-700015.
  • Collecting dealers must report their address-assignment method; DR-700020 for changes.
  • A new legal entity needs a new registration.
