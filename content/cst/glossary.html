Plain-language definitions of the terms used in this guide, with a link to the official source for each. Definitions are simplified for training. The statute or rule controls.

Address/Jurisdiction Database
The Department's database that assigns Florida addresses to local taxing jurisdictions. It is updated every six months, and changes take effect January 1 and July 1. {{cite:gt800011,dr700002}}
Alternative-period basis
A month-long reporting period other than a calendar month, ending on or after the 15th, that a dealer may elect on DR-700033 for at least 12 months. {{cite:tip-15a19-02}}
Annual Resale Certificate for Communications Services Tax (DR-700015)
The certificate a registered dealer gives sellers to buy communications services for resale. It is separate from the sales tax certificate and expires December 31. {{cite:cst-page,gt800011}}
Bundled transaction
Taxable and nontaxable services sold for one price. The whole price is taxable unless the nontaxable part can be reasonably identified from the dealer's books and records. {{cite:s202-11,dr700016n}}
Certified database
A dealer's or vendor's address database certified by the Department (application on DR-700012). Using one gives hold harmless protection and the 0.75% allowance. {{cite:dr700012,gt800011}}
Collection allowance
The part of the tax a dealer keeps for timely filing and payment: 0.75% with a qualifying address method, otherwise 0.25%; 0.75% for satellite providers. {{cite:gt800011,s202-28}}
Communications services
The transmission, conveyance or routing of voice, data, audio, video or other signals by any medium, including VoIP and video service. {{cite:s202-11}}
Communications services use tax
Tax a purchaser owes directly when the seller did not collect CST. Consumers file DR-700019; registered dealers report it on DR-700016. {{cite:cst-page,dr700019}}
Dealer
A person registered with the Department as a provider of communications services in Florida. {{cite:s202-11}}
Direct pay permit
Authority for a buyer to self-accrue and pay CST directly to the Department, granted for mostly inbound interstate service or when taxability is known only on use (DR-700030). {{cite:r12a19-030,gt800011}}
Direct-to-home satellite service
Satellite TV delivered to the customer's premises. Taxed at 9.07% + 2.37% = 11.44%, with no local tax and no residential exemption. {{cite:cst-page,s202-12}}
DR-700016
The Florida Communications Services Tax Return filed by dealers, with Page 1 and Schedules I to V. {{cite:dr700016n}}
Enhanced ZIP code (ZIP+4)
A nine-digit ZIP code. Assigning addresses by ZIP+4 with a reasonable methodology is one of the protected methods. {{cite:s202-22,r12a19-073}}
Florida communications services tax
The state and gross receipts parts of CST, 7.44% on most services, shown on bills separately from the local tax. {{cite:cst-page,gt800011}}
Gross receipts tax
The 2.37% and 0.15% parts of CST imposed by s. 203.01, F.S., and administered under Chapter 202. {{cite:cst-page,s203-01}}
Hold harmless
Protection from tax, penalty and interest caused by assigning an address to the wrong jurisdiction, for a dealer that uses due diligence with a protected method. {{cite:s202-22}}
Home service provider
The mobile provider with which the customer contracts; it must obtain and keep the customer's place of primary use. {{cite:s202-155}}
Information service
Generating, acquiring, storing, transforming, processing, retrieving or making available information via communications services, such as web hosting. Not a communications service. {{cite:s202-11,faq-1284}}
Local communications services tax
The tax a municipality, charter county or county levies on services billed to service addresses within it. {{cite:s202-19,gt800011}}
Mobile communications service
Wireless service such as cell phone plans, sourced to the place of primary use and never eligible for the residential exemption. {{cite:s202-11,s202-125}}
Place of primary use
For mobile service, the customer's residential or primary business street address within the provider's licensed service area. {{cite:s202-11}}
Prepaid calling arrangement
A phone card or qualifying prepaid mobile plan paid in advance in units. Its sale or recharge is taxed under the sales tax law, not CST. {{cite:s202-11,s212-05}}
Private communications service
Service giving exclusive or priority use of channels between termination points, such as a private line, with special apportionment rules. {{cite:s202-11,s202-12}}
Residential household exemption
The exemption of residential service from the 4.92% state tax and 0.15% gross receipts tax. The 2.37% and local tax still apply. {{cite:s202-125,cst-page}}
Sale for resale
A purchase that is resold, used as a component part of, or integrated into communications services sold at retail. It must be documented. {{cite:r12a19-060}}
Sales price
The total amount charged for communications services, including bundled items not separately itemized, without deduction for the dealer's expenses. {{cite:s202-11}}
Service address
The address that decides where a service is taxed: the equipment location, the central office for card calls, the place of primary use for mobile, or where video or satellite is received. {{cite:s202-11}}
Surtax conversion rate
The part of a jurisdiction's total local CST rate that reflects the county discretionary sales surtax, converted under s. 202.20(3). {{cite:s202-20,cst-rate-table}}
Total local rate
A jurisdiction's adopted local CST rate plus its county surtax conversion rate, as published by the Department. {{cite:s202-21,cst-rate-table}}
Transaction Resale Authorization Number
A number obtained online or by phone that documents a single sale for resale. {{cite:gt800011,cst-page}}
Transient public lodging establishment
A unit regularly rented to guests or rented more than three times a year for under 30 days. Its residences do not get the residential exemption. {{cite:tip-10a19-03,s202-125}}
Vendor Resale Authorization Number
A number from the online verification system, including batch uploads of up to 50,000 accounts, valid for sales to a buyer for the calendar year. {{cite:gt800011,cst-page}}
Video service
Video programming, including streaming, whether from a cable, telephone or other provider. Formerly called “cable service.” {{cite:gt800011,tip-12a19-04}}
Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP)
Telephone service carried over Internet protocol. It is a taxable communications service. {{cite:s202-11,cst-page}}
