Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

  • Complete Schedule I and its summary for several jurisdictions.
  • Carry the schedule totals to Page 1 and finish Lines 1 to 12.
  • Choose the right schedule for satellite sales, adjustments and multistate credits.
  • Correct tax reported to the wrong local jurisdiction on Schedule IV.

How the return fits together

Form DR-700016 has a one-page summary (Page 1) and supporting schedules. All supporting schedules are required to process the return. Leaving out Schedule I, or using an unapproved spreadsheet in its place, brings a $5,000 penalty. {{cite:dr700016n,s202-28}} Complete the schedules first, then Page 1. {{cite:dr700016n}}

Schedule I: state, gross receipts and local tax

Schedule I lists taxable sales and tax for each county and municipality where the dealer provides communications services. {{cite:dr700016n,cst-efile-guide}}

Schedule I columns
ColumnWhat goes in it
AThe local jurisdiction (in eFile, the county and then “State Portion – Col B” or the jurisdiction).
BTaxable sales subject to the 4.92% state tax and 0.15% gross receipts tax, including purchases taxed under a direct pay permit. Residential sales are left out.
CTaxable sales subject to the 2.37% gross receipts tax and local tax, including residential sales.
DThe jurisdiction's local tax rate (preprinted as a convenience).
ELocal tax due for that jurisdiction.

Schedule I must not contain negative numbers or other credits and adjustments. Eligible bad debt credits may be netted on it, as long as no result is below zero. {{cite:dr700016n}}

Summary of Schedule I

  • Column F: Line 1, total of Column B; Line 2, rate 0.0507; Line 3, tax due. Line 3 goes to Page 1, Line 1.
  • Column G: Line 4, total of Column C; Line 5, rate 0.0237; Line 6, gross receipts tax due. Line 6 goes to Page 1, Line 2.
  • Column H: Line 7, total of Column E, the local tax. It goes to Page 1, Line 3.

The tax entered should be the tax actually charged. “Tax due” is not simply a percentage of taxable sales, because each bill is rounded, and the amount reported must never be less than the tax actually charged. {{cite:dr700016n}}

Schedules II to V

  • Schedule II, direct-to-home satellite: Column A taxable sales, Column B the 0.1144 rate, Column C tax, which goes to Line 4. {{cite:dr700016n}}
  • Schedule III, satellite adjustments, and Schedule IV, other adjustments: credits and corrections for prior periods, tax paid on services that were resold, and moving tax between jurisdictions. Each adjustment also adjusts the collection allowance taken on the original return. {{cite:dr700016n,cst-efile-guide}}
  • Schedule V, multistate credits: credit for a like tax lawfully paid to another state, with proof of payment; total in Column H goes to Line 11. {{cite:dr700016n,s202-14}}

Moving local tax to the right jurisdiction (Schedule IV)

When local tax was reported in the wrong jurisdiction, the dealer moves it on Schedule IV. The Department's instructions work an example: $1,113.09 of local tax was reported in Jurisdiction A (1.10%) but belonged to Jurisdiction B (2.10%). The decrease in A's taxable sales is $1,113.09 ÷ 0.0110 = $101,190.00, shown in parentheses. The increase in B's taxable sales is $1,113.09 ÷ 0.0210 = $53,004.29. When tax moves to a jurisdiction with the same or a higher rate, the collection allowance adjustment is capped at the amount claimed on the original return. {{cite:dr700016n}}

A dealer using a protected method (Module 8) is held harmless from extra tax caused by the error. Without one, it may owe extra tax, plus penalty and interest. {{cite:s202-22,dr700016n}}

Bad debts

A dealer may take a credit for tax it paid on accounts that became worthless, within 12 months after the last day of the calendar year in which it charged off the bad debt on its federal income tax return. It can net the credit on Schedule I (or Schedule II for satellite) or report it on Schedule IV (or III). If a written-off account is later paid, the tax goes on the next return. {{cite:s202-29,tip-04a19-01,dr700016n,tip-10a19-04}}

Key takeaways

  • Schedule I: Column B for the 5.07% sales, Column C for 2.37% and local sales, Column E for local tax.
  • Page 1: Lines 1 to 3 from the Schedule I summary, Line 4 from Schedule II; Line 12 cannot be negative.
  • Missing Schedule I: $5,000 penalty.
  • Wrong-jurisdiction local tax is moved on Schedule IV.
