This guide teaches Florida communications services tax (CST) from the ground up. It starts with what the tax is and how its rates are built, and ends with filing the DR-700016 return, handling refunds and audits, and a capstone in which you complete a month of returns for a fictional provider.

Who this guide is for

  • New staff who answer questions about telephone, video, satellite and mobile taxes or review CST returns
  • Tax and billing staff at communications providers
  • Local government staff who work with local CST rates and the address database

An unofficial study aid

This guide is not legal advice and does not replace Chapter 202, Florida Statutes, Rule Chapter 12A-19, F.A.C., or the Department's publications. When anything here differs from the official source, the official source controls. {{cite:ch202,r12a19}}

How the guide is organized

The 17 modules are grouped into four levels. Each module has learning objectives, plain-language explanations, at least one worked example with a fictional business, a self-check, and a list of the official sources it relies on. Module 3 includes a calculator for the tax on one charge.
