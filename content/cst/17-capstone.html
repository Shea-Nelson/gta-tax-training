Learning objectives

This capstone pulls the whole guide together. You will:

  • Classify a month of charges as taxable, partly exempt, exempt or outside CST.
  • Build Schedule I, its summary and Page 1 of DR-700016.
  • Correct a prior-month situs error on Schedule IV.
  • Answer a customer's refund request on time.

The business

Fictional business. Rates are from the Department's January 2026 rate workbook. {{cite:cst-rate-table}} Mangrove Mobile, LLC is a registered CST dealer in Orange County. It uses the Department's Address/Jurisdiction Database, has reported that on DR-700020, and files electronically. {{cite:dr700020,gt800011}} Its customers are in two jurisdictions: Orlando (total local rate 5.52%) and the Town of Bay Lake (0.30%).

Step 4: fix last month's situs error

In November, Mangrove finds that $552.00 of October local tax was reported to Orlando (5.52%), but the customers' addresses are in unincorporated Orange County (5.28%). It uses the Department's method on Schedule IV of the November return. {{cite:dr700016n}}

Check your Schedule IV entries
  • Orlando: decrease in taxable sales $552.00 ÷ 0.0552 = ($10,000.00).
  • Orange County unincorporated: increase in taxable sales $552.00 ÷ 0.0528 = $10,454.55.
  • Because Mangrove used the Department's database, it is held harmless from tax, penalty and interest caused by the misassignment. Its collection allowance adjustment follows the Schedule IV instructions. {{cite:s202-22,dr700016n}}

Step 5: a customer's refund request

On December 3, 2026 an Orlando customer sends a signed written request: a home VoIP line was charged the 5.07% for the past 14 months. {{cite:s202-23}}

Check your answer
  • The request is timely: it is within 3 years of collection.
  • Mangrove has 30 days from the complete request to decide whether the tax was collected in error. Home VoIP qualifies for the residential partial exemption, so the 5.07% was not due.
  • It must refund or credit the amount within 45 days after deciding, and can then take a credit on a later return within 3 years after remitting the tax or 60 days after refunding the customer, whichever is later. {{cite:s202-23,s202-125}}

Key takeaways

  • Classify every charge before computing anything.
  • Column B excludes residential sales; Column C includes them.
  • Move misreported local tax on Schedule IV, and rely on hold harmless only with a protected method.
  • Answer refund requests on the statutory clock.
