Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

  • Recognize a prepaid calling arrangement, including prepaid mobile plans that qualify.
  • Explain why its sale is taxed under the sales tax law and not CST.
  • Source a prepaid sale made away from the seller's store.
  • Handle pay telephones, condominiums and parks that pass along service.

What a prepaid calling arrangement is

The law defines two kinds of prepaid calling arrangement: {{cite:s202-11}}

  1. a right to use communications services, other than mobile service, paid for in advance at a separately stated price, sold in predetermined units that decline with use, and consisting only of calls made by entering an access number or authorization code (the classic phone card); and
  2. a right to use mobile service paid for in advance and sold in predetermined units that expire or decline, if the right to use the service ends when the units run out unless more are bought, the buyer is not required to buy more, and any non-mobile use is limited to services through the same handset or device.

Units may be measured in usage, time, money or a combination. {{cite:s202-11}}

Outside CST, inside sales tax

The sale or recharge of a prepaid calling arrangement is excluded from the CST sales price. {{cite:s202-11,dr700016n}} Instead, it is taxed at 6% under the sales tax law, collected by the selling dealer at the time of sale. {{cite:s212-05}} Because it is a sales tax item, county discretionary sales surtax also applies where the sale occurs. {{cite:gt300015}}

The sales tax law adds three rules for these sales: {{cite:s212-05}}

  • The sale or recharge is treated as a sale of tangible personal property, whether or not a physical card is handed over.
  • If the sale or recharge does not take place at the dealer's place of business, it is deemed to take place at the customer's shipping address, or if nothing is shipped, at the customer's address or the location associated with the customer's mobile number.
  • No further sales tax or CST is due when the buyer uses the units to make calls or to get other services or products.

A retracted TIP

TIP 12ADM-02 on prepaid communications services was retracted on July 29, 2013. Do not rely on it. {{cite:tip-12adm-02}}

In the sales and use tax guide: the surtax on prepaid calling arrangements is due on the full price, with no $5,000 single-item limit. See SUT Module 6.

Other arrangements outside CST

  • Coin-operated pay telephones: calls paid by inserting coins into a public phone are not part of the CST sales price, so the operator does not collect CST on them. It may register to buy its lines for resale. {{cite:s202-11,faq-1281}}
  • Condominium and timeshare associations, RV and mobile home parks: if cable is included in the fees without a separately stated charge, the association or park does not collect CST from residents. {{cite:faq-1294,faq-1369}}
  • But a mobile home park that resells satellite service to residents as cable service is a dealer and must register and collect. {{cite:faq-1270}}

Island Wireless's prepaid sales

Fictional business; the 1% surtax is an example. {{cite:s212-05,s202-11,gt300015}}

  1. A customer buys a $25.00 refill for a prepaid mobile plan at Island Wireless's store in a county with a 1% surtax. The plan stops working when the units run out and the customer need not refill. It is a prepaid calling arrangement, so Island charges sales tax: $25.00 × 7% = $1.75. No CST.
  2. A customer recharges the same plan through Island's website. Nothing is shipped, so the sale is sourced to the customer's address or the location associated with the mobile number, and that county's surtax applies.
  3. A customer on a postpaid monthly mobile plan pays the bill in advance. The plan does not end when units run out, so it is not a prepaid calling arrangement. It is mobile service subject to CST.

Key takeaways

  • Phone cards and qualifying prepaid mobile plans are prepaid calling arrangements.
  • Their sale or recharge is taxed at 6% sales tax plus surtax, not CST.
  • Remote sales are sourced to the shipping address, customer address or mobile-number location.
  • Coin phones are outside CST; fee-included cable at condos and parks is not collected on.
