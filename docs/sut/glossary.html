Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used in this guide, with a link to the official source for each. Definitions are simplified for training. The statute or rule controls.

31 of 31 shown

Admission
A charge to enter or attend an event or place. Admissions are taxable unless specifically exempt, and the $5,000 surtax limit does not apply to them. [TIP 17A01-13] [GT-300015]
Address/Jurisdiction Database
The Department's database that assigns Florida addresses to taxing jurisdictions, so sellers can find the right surtax county. Explained in DR-700002. [DR-700002]
Amended return
A corrected return, completed as it should have been filed originally rather than showing only the changes. The quickest way to file one is online. [DR-15N]
Amusement Machine Certificate (DR-18C)
An annual certificate costing $30 per machine at each location. It expires June 30. [GT-800020]
Annual Resale Certificate (DR-13)
A certificate that lets a registered dealer buy or rent property or services tax-free when they will be resold or re-rented. It expires December 31. [SUT page] [Resale certificate page]
Business partner number
The Department's identification number for a taxpayer, used on correspondence and refund applications. [Refunds page]
Certificate of Registration (DR-11)
The certificate that authorizes a dealer to do business at the address shown. It must be displayed visibly. [GT-300015]
Certified Audit Program
A program in which a CPA firm reviews a taxpayer's compliance. In return, penalties are waived and part of the interest is abated. [GT-800065]
Collection allowance
A credit for timely electronic filers and payers: 2.5% of the first $1,200 of tax due, up to $30 per return. [DR-15N]
Consumer's Certificate of Exemption (DR-14)
A certificate issued to qualifying organizations, such as nonprofits, schools and government, so they can buy tax-exempt for their own use. Valid for five years. [DR-5]
Dealer
A person or business required to collect and remit sales tax. A dealer acts as an agent of the State of Florida. [GT-300015]
Discretionary sales surtax
A local option tax imposed by most counties, added to the state rate. It is collected based on where the item is delivered or the service is provided. [DR-15DSS] [SUT page]
Divisor
A Department-published factor used to back tax out of receipts that already include tax, such as from concessions, vending machines and bars. [GT-800003] [GT-800041]
DR-15 / DR-15EZ
The sales and use tax returns. The EZ version is for simpler businesses that do not, for example, rent transient accommodations or report sales in more than one county. [DR-15N] [DR-15EZN]
Effective tax rate (alcohol)
The rate multiplied by alcoholic beverage receipts when customers are not told that tax is included in the price. [GT-800046]
Estimated tax
Businesses that paid $200,000 or more in the prior state fiscal year must make an estimated payment with each return. [DR-15N]
Exempt sale
A sale on which no tax is due, such as a sale for resale or to a DR-14 holder. Exempt sales are reported in Column 2 of the DR-15. [DR-15N]
Filing frequency
How often a dealer files: monthly, quarterly, semiannually or annually, depending on its tax liability. [DR-15N]
Floating rate of interest
The interest rate on late tax. It is updated each January 1 and July 1 and published in a TIP. [Tax and interest rates page]
Gross sales
The total of all sales, taxable and exempt, before any deductions. Column 1 of the DR-15. [DR-15N]
Marketplace provider
A business that facilitates retail sales for marketplace sellers into Florida and must register and collect tax on them. [SUT page] [TIP 21A01-03]
Remote seller
An out-of-state seller with no physical presence in Florida. It must register if it made more than $100,000 in taxable remote sales into Florida in the prior calendar year. [SUT page] [TIP 21A01-03]
Rounding algorithm
Since July 1, 2021, tax is computed to the third decimal place and rounded up when the third decimal is greater than 4. It replaced the bracket system. [TIP 21A01-02]
Tangible personal property
Physical goods, such as most retail merchandise. A sale of tangible personal property in Florida is taxable at 6% plus any surtax unless an exemption applies. [TIP 03A01-17]
Tax Information Publication (TIP)
A Department notice explaining law changes or how the law applies to a topic. A TIP does not itself create rights or require compliance. [TIPs page] [TIP 10A01-22]
Technical Assistance Advisement (TAA)
A written statement of the Department's position on how a specific transaction is taxed. [GT-800022]
Transaction authorization number
A number from the Department that verifies a customer's resale certificate for one purchase. [Resale certificate page]
Transient rental
A rental of living, sleeping or housekeeping accommodations for six months or less, such as hotels and vacation rentals. Taxable, plus surtax and any local option transient rental tax, such as a tourist development tax. [SUT page] [GT-300015]
Use tax
Tax due on taxable items used, consumed or stored in Florida when sales tax was not paid, such as untaxed out-of-state purchases. [SUT page]
Vendor authorization number
An annual number that verifies a customer's resale status for the whole calendar year. [Resale certificate page]
Voluntary disclosure
A program for taxpayers not yet contacted by the Department to self-report unpaid tax. Penalties are generally waived, with a three-year lookback. [Voluntary disclosure page]
