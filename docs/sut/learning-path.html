Learning path

The guide takes about 8 hours 15 minutes, split into four levels. Work through the modules in order. Each one builds on the one before. At the end of each level, a checkpoint lists what you should be able to do before moving on. If you can't, go back to the module named in brackets.

Level 1: Beginner (about 70 minutes)

  1. Module 1: What sales and use tax is and who owes it
  2. Module 2: Registering a business
  3. Module 3: Taxable and exempt sales

Checkpoint: Explain the difference between sales tax and use tax [1]. Name the three state rates that aren't 6% [1]. Tell a new business owner which form to file to register and what they will receive [2]. Use the flowchart to classify a grocery item, a sandwich and a sale to a church [3].

Level 2: Intermediate (about 3 hours)

  1. Module 4: Taxable services
  2. Module 5: Use tax
  3. Module 6: Rates, discretionary sales surtax and rounding
  4. Module 7: Resale and exemption certificates
  5. Module 8: Filing and paying
    • Module 8A: eFile and Pay, step by step (companion to Module 8)

Checkpoint: Compute tax and surtax on a $7,000 item by hand and confirm it with the calculator [6]. Round a tax amount using the 2021 rule [6]. Pick the right one of the three ways to document a resale [7]. Fill in Lines A, B, 5, 10, 11 and 14 of a DR-15 and give its due and late dates [8]. Walk a taxpayer through logging in, filing and paying in eFile and Pay, and name the DR-659 deadline for a given month [8A].

Level 3: Advanced (about 2 hours 25 minutes)

  1. Module 9: Transient rentals and real property rentals
  2. Module 10: Motor vehicles, boats and aircraft
  3. Module 11: Food, beverages, restaurants and vending
  4. Module 12: Construction, manufacturing and agriculture
  5. Module 13: Admissions and amusements
  6. Module 14: Remote sellers, marketplace providers and delivery networks
  7. Module 15: Sales tax holidays and exemption periods

Checkpoint: Decide whether late-paid September 2025 office rent is taxable [9]. Source surtax for a boat sold to a buyer from another county [10]. Back the tax out of concession receipts with a divisor [11]. Decide whether an out-of-state seller must register [14]. Apply a holiday's price limits [15].

Level 4: Master (about 1 hour 40 minutes)

  1. Module 16: Records and audits
  2. Module 17: Penalties, interest, refunds and voluntary disclosure
  3. Module 18: Capstone

Checkpoint: Walk a dealer through an audit from the DR-840 to the protest deadlines [16]. Compute penalty and interest on a late return [17]. Complete the capstone without opening the answers [18].

Staying current

Florida's rates, surtaxes, exemptions and holidays change through new laws, usually effective January 1 or July 1. The Department announces changes in Tax Information Publications (TIPs). After finishing the guide, check the TIP index for anything issued after November 2026. [TIPs page] The Department's Taxpayer Education page has free tutorials and webinars. [Taxpayer Education page]
