Module 12: Construction, manufacturing and agriculture

Level: Advanced · About 20 minutes · Module 12 of 18

Learning objectives

  • Know where to find the contractor rules, and apply two current contractor TIPs: asphalt and public works refunds.
  • Document agricultural exemptions with a Florida Farm TEAM Card, an exemption certificate or an authorization number.
  • Recognize refund-only exemptions, such as the 2026 home-hardening exemption.

Scope note

Contractor taxation is detailed and fact-specific. The governing rule for contractors who improve real property is Rule 12A-1.051, F.A.C., in Rule Chapter 12A-1. This module covers the topics the harvested Department publications address directly. Have a subject-matter expert review real property contract questions. [Rule 12A-1, F.A.C.]

Contractors who make their own asphalt

Contractors who manufacture asphalt for their own use owe use tax, calculated as: [TIP 26A01-02]

  1. the cost of all ingredient materials × 6% plus surtax; plus
  2. the cost of transporting the ingredients to the plant × 6% plus surtax; plus
  3. each ton manufactured × the indexed tax: $1.19 per ton for July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027, up from $1.16.

Don't include costs on which tax was already paid to a third party. Asphalt used in federal, state or local government public works is exempt from the indexed tax. The tax is due on the DR-15 for the month the asphalt is made. [TIP 26A01-02] [TIP 25A01-10]

Ridge Paving (fictional)

In August 2026, Ridge Paving, in a county with no surtax, makes 1,000 tons of asphalt for a private parking lot. Its ingredients cost $40,000, with no tax paid to suppliers, and trucking them to the plant cost $5,000.

  • Ingredients: $40,000 × 6% = $2,400
  • Transportation: $5,000 × 6% = $300
  • Indexed tax: 1,000 tons × $1.19 = $1,190
  • Use tax due on the August return: $3,890

Method from TIP 26A01-02. [TIP 26A01-02]

Public works for state universities and colleges (refund)

Since July 1, 2026, tangible personal property sold to contractors working for a state university or Florida College System institution is exempt when it becomes part of public works the institution owns. The exemption works only through a refund to the institution, which files Forms DR-26S and DR-26PW each quarter with copies of invoices. [TIP 26A01-10] [DR-26PW]

Home-hardening products (refund)

Impact-resistant doors, garage doors and windows bought from July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2029 and installed on eligible homestead property are exempt, but only by a refund to the owner. Key limits: [TIP 26A01-09]

  • one property per owner, with a maximum refund of $500;
  • a site-built homestead with a just value of $700,000 or less;
  • claims filed between July 1, 2026 and September 30, 2029.

Sellers still charge tax at the point of sale. [TIP 26A01-09]

Agriculture

Florida exempts many items used by producers of agricultural commodities and aquaculture products, such as feed, fertilizers and pesticides, farm trailers, fencing and certain fuels. GT-800057 (N. 06/26) is the Department's 23-page guide to them. [GT-800057]

Since January 1, 2024, qualified farmers approved by the Department can present a Florida Farm TEAM Card instead of a paper exemption certificate for each purchase. [TIP 23A01-25] [TIP 24A01-01] A selling dealer documents the exempt sale by: [TIP 24A01-01]

  • keeping a copy of the farmer's TEAM Card (a dealer who accepts it in good faith is not liable for tax during the card's effective period); or
  • getting a transaction authorization number for that one sale online or at (877) 357-3725, or a vendor authorization number for regular customers.

Farmers without a card can still give an exemption certificate with their name and address, the reason for the exemption, and a signature. [TIP 24A01-01] The TEAM Card application is Form DR-1 TEAM. [Forms page]

Paying tax directly: self-accrual and direct pay

Form DR-16A is the Application for Self-Accrual Authority / Direct Pay Permit for sales and use tax. Ask an SME which businesses qualify. [Forms page]

Key takeaways

  • Contractor-made asphalt: tax on ingredients and transportation plus $1.19 per ton (July 2026 – June 2027).
  • Some exemptions are refund-only: public works for universities and colleges, and home-hardening products.
  • Farm exemptions can be documented with a TEAM Card copy, a certificate or an authorization number.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. What is the indexed tax per ton on contractor-made asphalt for July 2026 to June 2027?
Show the answer

Answer: $1.19. TIP 26A01-02 sets $1.19 per ton for July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027, up from $1.16.

2. A farmer shows a Florida Farm TEAM Card. How does the seller document the exempt sale?
Show the answer

Answer: Keep a copy of the card, or get an authorization number. TIP 24A01-01: keep a copy of the TEAM Card, or get a transaction or vendor authorization number.

3. How does a homeowner get the 2026 home-hardening exemption?
Show the answer

Answer: Only through a refund, up to $500. TIP 26A01-09: the exemption is refund-only, capped at $500 for one eligible property.
