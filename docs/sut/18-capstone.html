Module 18: Capstone: a full month for a fictional business

Level: Master · About 45 minutes · Module 18 of 18

Learning objectives

  • Classify a month of real-world-style transactions as taxable, exempt, or subject to use tax.
  • Compute state tax and surtax, including the $5,000 limit and a different-county delivery.
  • Complete the main DR-15 lines and the surtax lines, and apply the collection allowance.

The business

Harbor Light Gifts & Café is fictional. It is a registered dealer in a fictional Florida county with a 1% surtax. It is a monthly filer that files and pays electronically, and its October 2026 return is due November 1 and late after November 20, 2026. Work each step yourself before opening the answer.

Step 1: Classify each transaction

Show the classification
  1. Taxable sale: 6% plus 1% surtax (delivered in the home county). [GT-300015]
  2. Exempt sale for resale, documented with an authorization number (Module 7). [Resale certificate page]
  3. Exempt sale to a DR-14 holder paying with its own funds. [GT-800054]
  4. Taxable sale. State tax applies to the full $8,000. Surtax at 1.5% applies to the first $5,000 only, because delivery is in the other county. [GT-300015]
  5. Exempt bakery products: sealed and sold to go. [TIP 10A01-22]
  6. Taxable: food for consumption on the premises. [TIP 10A01-22]
  7. Use tax on an untaxed out-of-state purchase, reported on Line B. [SUT page] [DR-15N]
  8. Use tax on resale inventory used by the business, reported on Line B. [GT-300015]

Step 3: Complete the DR-15

Show the completed lines
  • Line A (Sales/Services/Electricity): gross sales $46,200; exempt sales $6,200 (transactions 2, 3 and 5); taxable amount $40,000; tax due $2,795.00 ($2,400 state + $395 surtax).
  • Line B (Taxable Purchases): taxable amount $2,800; tax due $196.00 ($168 state + $28 surtax).
  • Line 5, total tax due: $2,991.00. No lawful deductions, estimated tax or credit memos, so Lines 7 and 10 are also $2,991.00.
  • Line 11, collection allowance: 2.5% × $1,200 = $30.00, because Harbor Light files and pays electronically and on time.
  • Line 14, amount due: $2,961.00.
  • Line 15(a), exempt amount of items over $5,000: $3,000 (the sculpture's amount above $5,000).
  • Line 15(b), other taxable amounts not subject to surtax: $0.
  • Line 15(c), amounts subject to surtax at a rate different from the county rate: $5,000 (the part of the sculpture taxed at 1.5%).
  • Line 15(d), total surtax due: $423.00 ($395 on Line A + $28 on Line B).

Line definitions from DR-15N. [DR-15N] The collection allowance rule is from DR-15N and the Sales and Use Tax webpage. [SUT page]

For SME review

Line 15(c) asks for taxable amounts on which surtax is due at a different rate. This answer reports the $5,000 subject to the 1.5% surtax, with the $3,000 balance on Line 15(a). A subject-matter expert should confirm this before the guide is used in class.

Step 4: What if…?

Show the variations
  • Paid on November 25 instead: no collection allowance. The penalty is 10% of $2,991 = $299.10, which is above the $50 minimum. Interest is $2,991 × 5 days (November 21–25) × 0.000301370 = $4.51. [SUT page] [TIP 26ADM-02]
  • The church paid with the pastor's personal card: transaction 3 becomes taxable. [GT-800054]
  • The sculpture buyer picked it up at the store: surtax is at the home county's 1% on $5,000 ($50), and nothing goes on Line 15(c). [GT-300015]

You're ready when…

  • you can classify any transaction with the Module 3 flowchart;
  • you can compute tax and surtax by hand and confirm it with the calculator;
  • you can explain every DR-15 line to a new dealer.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. In the capstone, why is only $5,000 of the $8,000 sculpture subject to surtax?
Show the answer

Answer: The $5,000 single-item surtax limit. Surtax applies only to the first $5,000 of a single item of tangible personal property.

2. Why is Harbor Light's collection allowance $30 and not 2.5% of $2,991?
Show the answer

Answer: The allowance is 2.5% of only the first $1,200, so it can't exceed $30. 2.5% × $1,200 = $30, the maximum.

3. Which capstone items go on Line B?
Show the answer

Answer: The out-of-state shelving and the inventory used in the office. Untaxed out-of-state purchases and resale inventory used by the business owe use tax, reported on Line B.
