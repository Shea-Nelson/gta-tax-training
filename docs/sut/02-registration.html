Module 2: Registering a business

Level: Beginner · About 20 minutes · Module 2 of 18

Learning objectives

  • Decide whether a business must register for sales and use tax.
  • Choose between the online application, Form DR-1 and Form DR-1A.
  • List what a newly registered dealer receives and what each document is for.
  • Know which business changes need a notice and which need a new registration.

Who must register

Before starting a business in Florida, the owner must find out whether its activities or products are subject to sales and use tax. If they are, the business must register to collect sales tax or to pay use tax. [SUT page] The list of activities that require registration is in Module 1.

Businesses must register each location to collect, report and pay sales tax. [SUT page] Out-of-state remote sellers above the $100,000 threshold and marketplace providers also register; Module 14 covers them. [SUT page]

How to register

  • Online: the Department's online registration walks the applicant through an interview that helps determine which taxes apply. [GT-300015] [Registration page]
  • Paper: the Florida Business Tax Application, Form DR-1, with its instructions in Registering Your Business, Form DR-1N. [SUT page] [DR-1N]
  • Already registered and adding a location: a business that holds an active certificate of registration or reemployment tax account from an earlier DR-1 uses Form DR-1A to register an additional location or Florida rental property, or a location that moved from one Florida county to another. [SUT page] [DR-15N]

When registering, a business may also enroll to pay electronically, or to file and pay electronically, or it can enroll later. [GT-300015]

What a new dealer receives

After registering for sales and use tax, the business receives by mail: [GT-300015]

  • A Certificate of Registration (Form DR-11), which authorizes business at the address shown and must be displayed in a visible place at the business location. Flea market vendors and others with temporary locations must keep it with them at the temporary location.
  • A Florida Annual Resale Certificate for Sales Tax (Form DR-13) for tax-exempt purchases of items it will resell. A business registered only to pay use tax does not receive one. [SUT page]
  • An initial supply of returns (Form DR-15 or DR-15EZ), unless it files electronically.
  • The Discretionary Sales Surtax Information (Form DR-15DSS).
  • The New Dealer Guide (GT-800054). [GT-800054]

The certificate number is on the front of the DR-11 and the business partner number is on the back. Give both numbers when contacting the Department. [GT-300015] [GT-800054]

The first reporting period

The first reporting period starts on the date of the first taxable Florida activity entered on the application. If business does not start by that date, the dealer should call Taxpayer Services or file a return showing no tax due; otherwise it will receive a delinquency notice. [GT-300015] Returns are due even if the dealer did not receive paper returns. [GT-800054]

Changes after registration

What to do when the business changes

Business name, mailing address, or location address within the same county; business becomes active; closing or sellingNotify the Department; the quickest way is to update the account online. [SUT page]
Adding a location, or moving a location to another countyForm DR-1A (or online). [SUT page] [DR-15N]
Changing the legal entity, or changing ownershipA new registration online or a new Form DR-1. [SUT page] [DR-15N]
Closing or selling the businessFile a final return and pay all tax due within 15 days after closing or selling. The final return covers the period from the last return through the closing date. [DR-15N] [GT-800054]

Sources disagree: adding or moving a location

The New Dealer Guide (GT-800054, revised 07/21) says a business must submit a new registration or Form DR-1 to move to another county or add a location. [GT-800054] The newer DR-15 instructions (DR-15N, revised 10/25) and the current Sales and Use Tax webpage say to use Form DR-1A for those changes when the business already holds an active certificate. [DR-15N] [SUT page] This guide follows the newer sources.

Coastal Kayak Rentals

Fictional business. Maya opens Coastal Kayak Rentals as a new LLC. She rents kayaks by the hour from a shop in one county.

  1. Renting personal property is on the list of activities that require registration, so she must register before she starts. [SUT page]
  2. She registers online and enrolls to file and pay electronically at the same time. [GT-300015]
  3. She posts the DR-11 at the shop and keeps the DR-13 to buy kayaks for rent tax free (renting the kayaks out is a re-rental; see Module 7).
  4. A year later she opens a second shop in a different county. She already holds an active certificate, so she uses DR-1A for the new location. [SUT page]
  5. If she later converts the LLC to a corporation, that is a change of legal entity, so she must submit a new registration. [SUT page]

Key takeaways

  • Register before you start, and register each location.
  • New dealers get a DR-11 to display, a DR-13 resale certificate, returns, DR-15DSS and GT-800054.
  • Name and address changes are notices; new entity or new owner means a new registration.
  • File a final return within 15 days of closing or selling.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. A registered business opens a second location in another Florida county. Which form fits?
Show the answer

Answer: Form DR-1A, Application for Registered Businesses to Add a New Florida Location. A business with an active certificate uses DR-1A for an additional location or a move to another county (DR-15N R. 10/25 and the Sales and Use Tax webpage).

2. A sole proprietor incorporates the business. What must happen?
Show the answer

Answer: Submit a new registration, because the legal entity changed. A change of legal entity or ownership requires a new registration (online or DR-1).

3. Which document must be displayed in a visible place at the business location?
Show the answer

Answer: The Certificate of Registration (DR-11). The DR-11 authorizes business at the address shown and must be displayed visibly.

4. A business closes on June 10. When is its final return due?
Show the answer

Answer: Within 15 days after closing. A final return and all tax due must be filed within 15 days after closing or selling the business.
