Module 4: Taxable services

Level: Intermediate · About 20 minutes · Module 4 of 18

Learning objectives

  • Explain why most services are not taxed and name the service categories the Department lists as taxable.
  • Recognize when repair or cleaning work becomes a taxable sale of tangible personal property.
  • Source surtax on services and service warranties, and know the $5,000 limit does not apply.

The rule of thumb

Florida taxes services only when the law specifically names them. The Department's examples of taxable services are investigative and crime protection services, interior nonresidential cleaning services, and nonresidential pest control services. Anyone who provides taxable services must register. [SUT page] Selling service warranty contracts also requires registration. [SUT page]

Phone, internet-based calling, video and satellite services: these are communications services, taxed under Florida’s communications services tax, which has its own rates, registration and return. [GT-800011] See the Communications Services Tax guide. Prepaid calling arrangements are the exception: they are sold under the sales tax law (CST Module 11).

When “service” work is really a sale

When labor and materials are used to alter, remodel, maintain, adjust or repair tangible personal property, the taxable sales price includes both the labor and the materials. [TIP 03A01-17]

Fresh Fiber Cleaners (fictional)

Fresh Fiber cleans area rugs and sofas.

  • Cleaning only: not taxable.
  • Cleaning plus applying protector or deodorizer: the entire charge is taxable, whether or not the charges are listed separately, because tangible personal property is transferred.
  • Buying protector that becomes part of the rug: exempt for resale. Fresh Fiber gives its vendor a copy of its Annual Resale Certificate.
  • Buying machines, brushes and soaps: taxable to Fresh Fiber.
  • Carpet installed with tacks or glue: this guidance does not apply.

Source: TIP 03A01-17 (issued October 31, 2003). [TIP 03A01-17]

Shoreline Events hires security (fictional)

Shoreline Events hires a private guard company for a festival. The charge is a taxable protection service. It also hires off-duty sheriff's deputies through their agency. That detail is not taxable if every condition is met: [TIP 12A01-13]

  • the deputies perform duties their agency approved;
  • they are under the agency's direct command;
  • they are in an agency-authorized uniform;
  • they work where they have arrest jurisdiction.

The exclusion applies even if the work is called “off-duty” and even if the deputies are paid directly.

Surtax on services

  • Surtax on a taxable service is collected at the rate of the county where the service is provided. [GT-300015]
  • For a service warranty, surtax follows the county where the covered item is delivered or located. [GT-300015]
  • The $5,000 single-item surtax limit does not apply to taxable services or service warranties. [GT-300015]

Try it

Use the calculator in Module 6 and choose “A service, admission, transient rental, parking, or other charge with no surtax limit” for a $9,000 security contract.

Key takeaways

  • Most services are not taxed. Only services the law names are taxable.
  • Repair or maintenance work that transfers materials is taxable on labor and materials.
  • Surtax on services follows where the service is performed, with no $5,000 limit.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. Which of these services is taxable in Florida?
Show the answer

Answer: Nonresidential interior janitorial service. Nonresidential interior janitorial and cleaning services are among the taxable services reported on Line A; most other services are not taxed unless the law names them.

2. Does the $5,000 surtax limit apply to a $9,000 taxable security service contract?
Show the answer

Answer: No, the limit does not apply to taxable services. The $5,000 limit applies to items of tangible personal property, not to taxable services.

3. Where is surtax sourced for a taxable service?
Show the answer

Answer: Where the service is provided. Surtax on taxable services is collected at the rate of the county where the service is provided.
