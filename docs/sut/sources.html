Sources and method

This page explains how the guide was researched, what was collected, and what could not be collected. Every factual statement in the modules links to the official Department page or document it comes from.

Method

  1. Polite harvest (November 2026). A script crawled public floridarevenue.com pages related to sales and use tax and downloaded the linked forms, brochures, TIPs, tutorial transcripts and guides. It identified itself in its user agent, waited 1.2 seconds between requests, used no sign-in, and obeyed robots.txt. Each file is recorded in a manifest with its URL, type, title, form or TIP number, revision date, fetch time and SHA-256 hash.
  2. Reading. PDFs and Word files were converted to text. The modules were written from the Department's own guides (GT-300015 Business Owner's Guide, DR-15N and DR-15EZN instructions, GT-800054 New Dealer Guide, the industry brochures) and the TIPs. Department webpages were used to confirm current rules.
  3. Citation rules. No rate, threshold or date appears without a link to its source. Statutes and rules are linked only, never paraphrased as law. Where two sources disagree, the guide uses the newer one and flags the conflict.
  4. Examples. All businesses in examples are fictional. No taxpayer or confidential data was used. Examples marked “Department example” are reproduced from Department publications.

The harvest was deliberately broad: it followed every link on the sales and use tax pages and in the forms index. Some files, such as property tax and reemployment tax forms, are not about sales and use tax. They are flagged in the manifest and not used in the guide. The source files total more than 200 MB, so the repository holds the manifest (JSON and CSV) and the harvest scripts, not the files. Every file can be downloaded again from its recorded URL.

What could not be collected

  • Excluded by robots.txt: prior-year forms (paths under /Forms_library/20…): 105 files, each tried twice, so 210 requests were skipped. The same robots.txt also excludes the sales tax holiday landing pages (back-to-school, Freedom Month, hunting season, disaster preparedness, tool time) and /dor/ pages, including /dor/training, so the crawler never requested them. The guide relies on the TIPs for holidays.
  • Not found (404): brochures GT-800001, GT-800036, GT-800037 and GT-800053, which other documents still reference; the DR-1215 insert; and one property tax document. One archived fuel-tax specification required sign-in (401) and was skipped. No request was refused with a 403. The brochures and the DR-1215 insert were checked again on November 22, 2026 and still return 404.
  • Follow-up for Module 8A (November 22, 2026): the eFile and Pay pages, user guides and FAQ already harvested were re-read. One missing public guide was fetched the same polite way: the Secure File Transmission (Bulk Filer) guide. [Secure File Transmission guide] The software developer information page linked from GT-800002 returned 404. The login and payment portals themselves were never opened or signed in to.
  • Referenced but not in the current forms index: GT-800030 (motor vehicles) and GT-800060 (resale certificates).
  • Questions and answers: the Department's FAQ pages load their answers dynamically, so their text was not captured. Use the FAQ search on floridarevenue.com directly. [DOR FAQ]
  • 2027 surtax table: TIPs issued November 20, 2026 announce surtax changes for January 1, 2027, but the 2027 DR-15DSS was not available when the harvest ran or when the forms page was checked again on November 22, 2026. [TIP 26A01-14]
