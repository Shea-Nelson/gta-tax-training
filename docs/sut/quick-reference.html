Quick reference

The figures most often needed, each linked to its source. They are current as of the sources retrieved in November 2026. Check the TIP index for later changes. [TIPs page] This page prints on its own.

Rates

  • General state rate: 6% [SUT page]
  • New mobile homes: 3% (since July 1, 2022) [TIP 22A01-12]
  • Amusement machine receipts: 4% [GT-800020]
  • Electricity: 6.95% [GT-300015]
  • Commercial rent: repealed for periods on or after October 1, 2025; 2% for earlier periods [TIP 25A01-04]
  • County surtax: see the DR-15DSS for the current year [DR-15DSS (2026)]

Surtax rules

  • Goods: county where delivered [GT-300015]
  • Services: county where performed [GT-300015]
  • Vehicles, mobile homes, boat-and-trailer combos: buyer's county on the title or registration [SUT page] [TIP 24A01-11]
  • $5,000 limit: per single item of tangible personal property only, not admissions, services, warranties, transient rentals or parking [GT-300015]

Rounding

  • Carry tax to the third decimal and round up if the third decimal is greater than 4 (since July 1, 2021) [TIP 21A01-02]
  • Rounding cash totals to the nearest nickel: see TIP 26A01-03 [TIP 26A01-03]

Filing

  • Due the 1st; late after the 20th of the following month [DR-15N]
  • E-payment: start it and get a confirmation number by 5 p.m. ET on the business day before the 20th [DR-15N]
  • Frequency by annual tax: over $1,000 monthly; $501–$1,000 quarterly; $101–$500 semiannual; $100 or less annual [SUT page] [GT-300015]
  • E-filing required if $5,000 or more was paid in the prior state fiscal year [SUT page], or for consolidated filers [GT-800002]
  • eFile and Pay: floridarevenue.com/taxes/filepay, then Sales and Use Tax; enrolled users log in with User ID and Password, guests use Certificate Number and Business Partner Number (no leading zeros) [TIP 25A01-19]. Step by step: Module 8A
  • 2026 e-payment deadlines (DR-659): Jan 16, Feb 19, Mar 19, Apr 17, May 19, Jun 18, Jul 17, Aug 19, Sep 18, Oct 19, Nov 19, Dec 18 [DR-659]
  • ACH debit can be canceled until 5 p.m. ET the day submitted; card payments can't be [eFile FAQ]
  • Estimated tax if $200,000 or more was paid in the prior state fiscal year [DR-15N]
  • File a return for every period, even when no tax is due [DR-15N]
  • Final return within 15 days of closing [GT-300015]

Allowance, penalty, interest

  • Collection allowance: 2.5% of the first $1,200 of Line 10, maximum $30, electronic and on time only [DR-15N]
  • Late penalty: 10% of Line 10, minimum $50 [DR-15N]
  • E-file or e-pay failure: $10 each [SUT page]
  • Interest: 11% (daily factor 0.000301370) from January 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027 [TIP 26ADM-02] [TIP 26ADM-06]

Certificates

  • Annual Resale Certificate (DR-13): expires December 31 [SUT page]
  • Consumer's Certificate of Exemption (DR-14): valid five years [DR-5]
  • Verification line: (877) 357-3725 [TIP 24A01-01]
  • Keep copies of certificates for three years [Resale certificate page]

Thresholds and limits

  • Remote sellers: over $100,000 in taxable remote sales in the prior calendar year [TIP 21A01-03]
  • Transient rental: six months or less [SUT page]
  • Records: at least three years [GT-300015]
  • Refund claims: within three years of payment [Refunds page]
  • Amusement machine certificate: $30 per machine per year, expires June 30 [GT-800020]

Audit deadlines

  • DR-840 notice period: 60 days [GT-800022]
  • DR-1215 response: 30 days [GT-800022]
  • Protest of a Notice of Proposed Assessment: 60 days informal, 120 days formal [GT-800022]

Contacts

  • Taxpayer Services: 850-488-6800 [GT-800022]
  • Telefile: 800-550-6713 [DR-15N]
  • Debit-block company ID for sales and use tax: 7596001874 [GT-800012]
  • Written questions: fdortaxpayerservices@floridarevenue.com [TIP 25A01-19]
  • Refund process: 850-617-8585 [Refunds page]
