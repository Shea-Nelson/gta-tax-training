Module 17: Capstone: a month of CST for a fictional provider

Level: Master · About 45 minutes · Module 17 of 17

Learning objectives

This capstone pulls the whole guide together. You will:

  • Classify a month of charges as taxable, partly exempt, exempt or outside CST.
  • Build Schedule I, its summary and Page 1 of DR-700016.
  • Correct a prior-month situs error on Schedule IV.
  • Answer a customer's refund request on time.

The business

Fictional business. Rates are from the Department's January 2026 rate workbook. [CST rate workbook] Mangrove Mobile, LLC is a registered CST dealer in Orange County. It uses the Department's Address/Jurisdiction Database, has reported that on DR-700020, and files electronically. [DR-700020] [GT-800011] Its customers are in two jurisdictions: Orlando (total local rate 5.52%) and the Town of Bay Lake (0.30%).

Check your classifications
  • Items 1 and 2: fully taxable (5.07% + 2.37% + local). Mobile service gets no residential exemption. [s. 202.125, F.S.] [GT-800011]
  • Item 3: residential partial exemption, so 2.37% + local only. [s. 202.125, F.S.]
  • Item 4: fully taxable at Bay Lake's rate.
  • Items 5 and 6: not communications services (Internet access, equipment, late payment charges). Check sales tax for the phones. [s. 202.11, F.S.]
  • Item 7: a prepaid calling arrangement, taxed under the sales tax law, not CST. [s. 212.05, F.S.] [s. 202.11, F.S.]
  • Item 8: sale for resale, properly documented. [GT-800011] [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.]
  • Item 9: exempt government sale, documented by the customer application. [Rule 12A-19.042, F.A.C.]

Step 4: fix last month's situs error

In November, Mangrove finds that $552.00 of October local tax was reported to Orlando (5.52%), but the customers' addresses are in unincorporated Orange County (5.28%). It uses the Department's method on Schedule IV of the November return. [DR-700016N]

Check your Schedule IV entries
  • Orlando: decrease in taxable sales $552.00 ÷ 0.0552 = ($10,000.00).
  • Orange County unincorporated: increase in taxable sales $552.00 ÷ 0.0528 = $10,454.55.
  • Because Mangrove used the Department's database, it is held harmless from tax, penalty and interest caused by the misassignment. Its collection allowance adjustment follows the Schedule IV instructions. [s. 202.22, F.S.] [DR-700016N]

Step 5: a customer's refund request

On December 3, 2026 an Orlando customer sends a signed written request: a home VoIP line was charged the 5.07% for the past 14 months. [s. 202.23, F.S.]

Check your answer
  • The request is timely: it is within 3 years of collection.
  • Mangrove has 30 days from the complete request to decide whether the tax was collected in error. Home VoIP qualifies for the residential partial exemption, so the 5.07% was not due.
  • It must refund or credit the amount within 45 days after deciding, and can then take a credit on a later return within 3 years after remitting the tax or 60 days after refunding the customer, whichever is later. [s. 202.23, F.S.] [s. 202.125, F.S.]

Key takeaways

  • Classify every charge before computing anything.
  • Column B excludes residential sales; Column C includes them.
  • Move misreported local tax on Schedule IV, and rely on hold harmless only with a protected method.
  • Answer refund requests on the statutory clock.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. In the capstone, why does the Orlando mobile plan revenue go in Column B?
Show the answer

Answer: Mobile service never gets the residential exemption. Mobile service is fully taxable, including the 5.07%.

2. Why are the prepaid refill cards left off the CST return?
Show the answer

Answer: They are taxed under the sales tax law instead. The sale of prepaid calling arrangements is taxed under s. 212.05, not CST.

3. Mangrove's Line 5 is $8,716.80. What is its collection allowance if on time?
Show the answer

Answer: $65.38. 0.0075 × $8,716.80 = $65.376, rounded to $65.38.

4. Mangrove reported $552.00 of local tax to Orlando (5.52%) that belonged elsewhere. What is the decrease in Orlando's taxable sales on Schedule IV?
Show the answer

Answer: $10,000.00. $552.00 ÷ 0.0552 = $10,000.00, shown in parentheses.
