Learning path

The guide takes about 7½ hours, split into four levels. Work through the modules in order. At the end of each level, a checkpoint lists what you should be able to do before moving on. If you can't, go back to the module named in brackets.

Level 1: Beginner (about 1 hour 35 minutes)

  1. Module 1: What communications services tax is
  2. Module 2: The Florida CST rates and the residential exemption
  3. Module 3: Local CST and computing the tax on a bill
  4. Module 4: Registering as a communications services dealer

Checkpoint: Name the three parts of CST and what must appear on a bill [1]. Give the 7.44% and 11.44% rates and what the residential exemption removes [2]. Build a total local rate and round a tax amount, then check it with the calculator [3]. Tell a new provider how to register and what it will receive [4].

Level 2: Intermediate (about 2 hours 35 minutes)

  1. Module 5: Which services are taxable
  2. Module 6: Sales price, bundles and charges on the bill
  3. Module 7: Where a service is taxed: the service address
  4. Module 8: The Address/Jurisdiction Database and the collection allowance
  5. Module 9: Exempt sales and how to document them
  6. Module 10: Sales for resale

Checkpoint: Classify a cable bill line by line [5, 6]. Source a mobile plan, a satellite subscription and an interstate private line [7]. Pick the collection allowance for a dealer's address method [8]. Document a county government account and a church account [9]. Choose among a certificate copy, a Transaction Resale Authorization Number and a Vendor Resale Authorization Number [10].

Level 3: Advanced (about 2 hours 5 minutes)

  1. Module 11: Prepaid calling arrangements and other services outside CST
  2. Module 12: Use tax and direct pay permits
  3. Module 13: Filing and paying, including the new eFile system
  4. Module 14: The DR-700016 return, line by line
  5. Module 15: Local rate changes and effective dates

Checkpoint: Explain why a prepaid refill gets sales tax, not CST [11]. Tell a household which use tax return to file and when [12]. Give a month's due date, late date and electronic payment deadline [13]. Complete Schedule I and Page 1 for two jurisdictions [14]. Decide which rate applies to a bill dated after a January 1 change [15].

Level 4: Master (about 1 hour 15 minutes)

  1. Module 16: Refunds, bad debts, audits and penalties
  2. Module 17: Capstone: a month of CST for a fictional provider

Checkpoint: Walk a customer refund request through its deadlines and compute a late penalty [16]. Complete the capstone without opening the answers [17].

Keep the quick reference and glossary open as you study. Sources for the rules in each module are listed at the end of that module. [CST page]
