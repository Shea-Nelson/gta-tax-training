Sources and method

This page explains how the guide was researched, what was collected, and what could not be collected. Every factual statement in the modules links to the official page or document it comes from.

Method

  1. Polite harvest (November 21–22, 2026). A script crawled public floridarevenue.com pages about communications services tax, the CST pages of the Address/Jurisdiction Database site, the TIP index, Chapter 202 and related sections on Online Sunshine, and Rule Chapter 12A-19 on flrules.org. It identified itself in its user agent, waited 1.5 seconds between requests, made only GET requests, used no sign-in, and obeyed each site's robots.txt. Each file is recorded in a manifest with its URL, type, title, form or TIP number, revision date, fetch time and SHA-256 hash.
  2. Reading. PDFs, Word files and spreadsheets were converted to text. The modules were written from the statute and rule, the return instructions (DR-700016N), the brochure GT-800011, the CST webpage, the eFile guide, the rate workbook and the TIPs.
  3. Citation rules. No rate, threshold or date appears without a link to its source. Where sources disagree, the guide follows the newer one and says so below.
  4. Examples. All businesses and people in examples are fictional. Local rates in examples are real rates from the Department's January 2026 workbook. Examples marked “Department example” are reproduced from Department publications.

The repository holds the manifest (source/cst/manifest.json and .csv) and the harvest scripts, not the files. Every file can be downloaded again from its recorded URL. A few collected forms (DR-133, DR-15SW, DR-15SWN and the gross receipts eFile guide) mention gross receipts but are not about CST; they are listed as related on the form finder and not used in the modules.

What could not be collected

  • Questions and answers: the Department's FAQ list pages its results with a form post, which a read-only crawler does not send. Only the 10 CST answers shown on the first page were collected. Use the FAQ search on floridarevenue.com for the rest. [CST FAQ]
  • 2026 TIPs: the TIP index page for 2026 returned “not found,” and no 2026 CST TIP was found elsewhere. The newest CST TIPs collected are TIP 25A19-01 and 25A19-02. [TIPs page]
  • Rates for 2027: the rate workbook collected is dated January 2026 and lists no upcoming rates. [CST rate workbook]
  • Interest from January 1, 2027: not yet published when the harvest ran. [Tax and interest rates page]
  • No request was refused (no 403 responses), no sign-in was attempted, and no robots.txt exclusion applied to the pages needed.
