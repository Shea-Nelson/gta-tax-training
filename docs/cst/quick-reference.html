Quick reference

The figures most often needed, each linked to its source. They are current as of the sources retrieved in November 2026. Check the TIP index and rate table for later changes. [TIPs page] [CST rate table] This page prints on its own.

Rates

  • State CST 4.92% + gross receipts 0.15% = 5.07% “state tax” [CST page]
  • Gross receipts 2.37%; Florida CST total 7.44% [CST page]
  • Direct-to-home satellite: 9.07% + 2.37% = 11.44%, no local tax [CST page]
  • Residential landline or VoIP: 2.37% + local (not mobile, video, satellite or transient lodging) [s. 202.125, F.S.]
  • Local: adopted rate + surtax conversion rate, by jurisdiction; 0.30% to 7.70% in January 2026 [CST rate workbook]

Rounding

  • Carry to three decimals; round up when the third decimal is 5 or more [TIP 11A19-01]
  • Round local tax separately from Florida CST [TIP 11A19-01]
  • Per item allowed if the total is at least the invoice-level result [TIP 11A19-01]

Deadlines

  • Return and payment due the 1st, late after the 20th; file even with no tax due [CST page]
  • Electronic payment: confirmation number by 5:00 p.m. ET the business day before the 20th (DR-659) [CST page] [DR-659]
  • Mandatory electronic filing: $5,000 or more paid in the prior July–June fiscal year [CST page]
  • DR-700019 use tax: periods ending June 30 and December 31, late after July 20 and January 20 [DR-700019]
  • Local rate change: notice by September 1; effective for bills dated on or after January 1; no increases before January 1, 2031 [s. 202.21, F.S.] [s. 202.19, F.S.]
  • Address database changes: January 1 and July 1 [DR-700002]

Allowance and penalties

  • Collection allowance 0.75% (qualifying address method, or satellite) or 0.25% [GT-800011]
  • Late: 10% per 30 days or fraction, up to 50% [CST page]
  • Interest July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027: 11% a year, daily factor 0.000301370 [TIP 26ADM-02] [TIP 26ADM-06]
  • No Schedule I: $5,000 per return. Wrong jurisdiction without a protected method: 10%, up to $10,000 per return [s. 202.28, F.S.]

Resale and exemptions

  • CST resale certificate DR-700015, expires December 31 [CST page]
  • Verify online or at 877-357-3725; batch up to 50,000 accounts [GT-800011]
  • Exempt: resale, Internet access, governments (direct pay), 501(c)(3) religious and educational institutions, qualifying homes for the aged [CST page]
  • Direct pay permit: DR-700030; five years; renew on DR-700032 [Rule 12A-19.030, F.A.C.]

Key forms

  • DR-1 registration; DR-700020 address method [CST page] [DR-700020]
  • DR-700016 return; DR-700016N instructions [DR-700016N]
  • DR-700022 and DR-700025 address database changes and objections [DR-700022] [DR-700025]
  • DR-700021 local rate change notice [DR-700021]
  • DR-841 copy of a return filed before June 1, 2026 [CST eFile and Pay page]

Help

  • Taxpayer Services: 850-488-6800 [GT-800011]
  • eFile help: fdortaxpayerservices@floridarevenue.com [CST eFile and Pay page]
  • Address lookup: the Address/Jurisdiction Database [Address/Jurisdiction Database]
