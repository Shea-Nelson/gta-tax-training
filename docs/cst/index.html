Florida Communications Services Tax training guide

This guide teaches Florida communications services tax (CST) from the ground up. It starts with what the tax is and how its rates are built, and ends with filing the DR-700016 return, handling refunds and audits, and a capstone in which you complete a month of returns for a fictional provider.

Who this guide is for

  • New staff who answer questions about telephone, video, satellite and mobile taxes or review CST returns
  • Tax and billing staff at communications providers
  • Local government staff who work with local CST rates and the address database

An unofficial study aid

This guide is not legal advice and does not replace Chapter 202, Florida Statutes, Rule Chapter 12A-19, F.A.C., or the Department's publications. When anything here differs from the official source, the official source controls. [Ch. 202, F.S.] [Rule 12A-19, F.A.C.]

How the guide is organized

The 17 modules are grouped into four levels. Each module has learning objectives, plain-language explanations, at least one worked example with a fictional business, a self-check, and a list of the official sources it relies on. Module 3 includes a calculator for the tax on one charge.

Levels and modules
LevelModulesYou will be able to
Beginner1 to 4Explain what CST is, apply the state, gross receipts, satellite and local rates, round correctly, and register a dealer.
Intermediate5 to 10Decide what is taxable, handle bundles, source a service to the right address and jurisdiction, and document exempt and resale sales.
Advanced11 to 15Handle prepaid calling, use tax and direct pay permits, file and pay in the new eFile system, complete DR-700016, and track rate changes.
Master16 to 17Process refunds, compute penalties, explain an audit, and complete and correct a full return.

All modules

  • Module 1: What communications services tax is

    The three parts of the tax, who collects it, separately stating it on bills, and how the money flows to the state and local governments.

    Beginner · about 20 minutes

  • Module 2: The Florida CST rates and the residential exemption

    The 4.92%, 0.15% and 2.37% pieces, the 7.44% total, the 11.44% satellite rate, and the residential household exemption.

    Beginner · about 25 minutes

  • Module 3: Local CST and computing the tax on a bill

    Who levies local CST, how a jurisdiction's total rate is built, the rounding rule, and a calculator.

    Beginner · about 30 minutes

  • Module 4: Registering as a communications services dealer

    Who must register, who need not, Form DR-1, what a new dealer receives, and keeping the account current.

    Beginner · about 20 minutes

  • Module 5: Which services are taxable

    The definition of communications services, the list of taxable services, and the services and charges outside the tax.

    Intermediate · about 30 minutes

  • Module 6: Sales price, bundles and charges on the bill

    What is in and out of the taxable sales price, always-taxable charges, and how bundled taxable and nontaxable services are treated.

    Intermediate · about 25 minutes

  • Module 7: Where a service is taxed: the service address

    The service address rules for fixed, card-billed, video, satellite and mobile service, and the private line rules.

    Intermediate · about 25 minutes

  • Module 8: The Address/Jurisdiction Database and the collection allowance

    Assigning addresses to jurisdictions, hold harmless protection, certified databases, change requests, and the 0.75% or 0.25% allowance.

    Intermediate · about 25 minutes

  • Module 9: Exempt sales and how to document them

    Government, religious and educational institutions, homes for the aged, Internet access providers, and the records a dealer must keep.

    Intermediate · about 25 minutes

  • Module 10: Sales for resale

    The Annual Resale Certificate for CST, Transaction and Vendor Resale Authorization Numbers, and what happens without proper documentation.

    Intermediate · about 25 minutes

  • Module 11: Prepaid calling arrangements and other services outside CST

    Why prepaid calling is taxed under the sales tax law instead, and where the line falls for pay telephones, condominiums and parks.

    Advanced · about 15 minutes

  • Module 12: Use tax and direct pay permits

    When a purchaser owes CST directly, the DR-700019 consumer return, and self-accrual under a direct pay permit.

    Advanced · about 20 minutes

  • Module 13: Filing and paying, including the new eFile system

    Due dates, electronic filing rules, the June 2026 eFile system, Direct File, payment deadlines and the alternative filing period.

    Advanced · about 30 minutes

  • Module 14: The DR-700016 return, line by line

    Page 1 Lines 1 to 12, Schedule I and its summary, Schedules II to V, and a fully worked return.

    Advanced · about 40 minutes

  • Module 15: Local rate changes and effective dates

    How and when local rates change, notice deadlines, the freeze through 2030, database update dates, and reading the rate table.

    Advanced · about 20 minutes

  • Module 16: Refunds, bad debts, audits and penalties

    The customer refund procedure, dealer credits, bad debt, penalties, interest and what happens in a CST audit.

    Master · about 30 minutes

  • Module 17: Capstone: a month of CST for a fictional provider

    Classify a month of charges, compute every tax, complete Schedule I and Page 1, and correct a situs error.

    Master · about 45 minutes

Important notes on currency

Several things changed recently: CST moved to a new eFile and Pay system on June 1, 2026 [CST eFile and Pay page], the freeze on local rate increases was extended to January 1, 2031 [TIP 25A19-01], and local rates in Martin and Palm Beach counties changed on January 1, 2026 [TIP 25A19-02]. The sources were retrieved in November 2026. Check the Department's Tax Information Publications and rate table for later changes.
