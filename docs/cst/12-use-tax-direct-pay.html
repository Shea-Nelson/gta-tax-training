Module 12: Use tax and direct pay permits

Level: Advanced · About 20 minutes · Module 12 of 17

Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

  • Decide when a purchaser owes CST use tax and which return to use.
  • Give the DR-700019 filing periods and due dates.
  • Explain the two kinds of direct pay permit and the annual limits for interstate permits.

Use tax: when the seller does not collect

If a dealer does not collect CST from a customer, the customer is responsible for paying communications services use tax. [CST page] [s. 202.151, F.S.] This most often happens when a business or household buys taxable service from a provider that is not registered in Florida. [TIP 02A19-09] [GT-800011]

  • Registered dealers report use tax they owe on their own DR-700016 return; they should not use the consumer form. [DR-700019] [CST page]
  • Everyone else files Form DR-700019, Communications Services Use Tax Return. [DR-700019] [Rule 12A-19.020, F.A.C.]

DR-700019 basics

The DR-700019 is filed semiannually. Returns for the period ending June 30 are due July 1 and late after July 20; returns for the period ending December 31 are due January 1 and late after January 20, moving to the next business day when the 20th is a weekend or holiday. [DR-700019] [Rule 12A-19.020, F.A.C.] The same rates and residential partial exemption apply as on a dealer's bill. [DR-700019]

Example: Sawgrass Accounting's out-of-state VoIP

Fictional business and rate. Sawgrass Accounting, a firm in a city with a 5.22% local rate, buys a $300.00 a month VoIP plan from an out-of-state company that does not collect Florida CST. From January to June it owes use tax on $1,800.00: [DR-700019] [CST page]

  • 5.07%: $91.26; 2.37%: $42.66; local 5.22%: $93.96. Total $227.88.
  • It files DR-700019 for the period ending June 30 by July 20.

Direct pay permits (self-accrual)

A buyer of communications services can apply for a direct pay permit to accrue and pay the tax on its purchases directly to the Department, instead of paying it to the seller. Two situations qualify: [Rule 12A-19.030, F.A.C.] [GT-800011] [s. 202.12, F.S.]

  1. most of the communications services it buys are for communications that originate outside Florida and terminate in Florida; or
  2. the taxable status of the services will be known only when they are used.

Key rules: [Rule 12A-19.030, F.A.C.] [DR-700030] [DR-700032]

  • Apply on Form DR-700030. A permit is issued to a single legal entity, not to a group of affiliates.
  • The holder must be registered for CST (DR-700014); if not, it files DR-1 with the application.
  • A permit lasts five years. The Department sends a renewal notice, DR-700032, 60 days before it expires.

For holders of a permit for interstate services, the law caps the tax: the state and gross receipts tax on interstate services that originate outside Florida and terminate in Florida may not exceed $100,000 per calendar year, and a local tax on such services billed to a service address in a jurisdiction may not exceed $25,000 per calendar year. No refund is given for tax paid before the permit was received. [s. 202.12, F.S.] [s. 202.19, F.S.] Permit holders get no collection allowance on tax they accrue but do not collect from customers. [DR-700016N]

Key takeaways

  • When the seller does not collect, the buyer owes use tax.
  • Registered dealers use DR-700016; others use DR-700019, filed for June and December periods, late after the 20th.
  • Direct pay permits: mostly inbound interstate service, or taxability known only on use. Apply on DR-700030, renew on DR-700032.
  • Interstate permit caps: $100,000 a year state, $25,000 a year local.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. A consumer buys taxable service from a seller that does not collect Florida CST. What should the consumer file?
Show the answer

Answer: DR-700019. Consumers report use tax on DR-700019; registered dealers use DR-700016.

2. When is a DR-700019 for the period ending June 30 late?
Show the answer

Answer: After July 20. It is due July 1 and late after July 20.

3. Which situation can qualify for a CST direct pay permit?
Show the answer

Answer: Most services bought originate outside Florida and terminate in Florida. The other basis is when taxability is known only on use.

4. How long does a direct pay permit last?
Show the answer

Answer: Five years. Rule 12A-19.030 sets a five-year term with renewal on DR-700032.
