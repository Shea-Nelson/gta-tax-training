Module 10: Sales for resale

Level: Intermediate · About 25 minutes · Module 10 of 17

Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

  • Define a sale for resale of communications services.
  • Document a resale with a certificate, a Transaction Resale Authorization Number or a Vendor Resale Authorization Number.
  • Explain the consequences of a resale sale made without proper documentation.

What counts as a sale for resale

A sale for resale happens when a buyer purchases communications services and then resells them, uses them as a component part of communications services it sells at retail, or integrates them into communications services it sells at retail. [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.] [s. 202.11, F.S.] A sale for resale is excluded from CST only when it is made in strict compliance with the resale rule. [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.] [s. 202.16, F.S.]

The Annual Resale Certificate for Communications Services Tax

Each new dealer receives a Certificate of Registration (DR-700014) and a CST Annual Resale Certificate (DR-700015), and each active registered dealer gets a new resale certificate each calendar year. [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.] [GT-800011] Key facts: [GT-800011] [CST page] [Print certificate page]

  • It is separate from the sales and use tax resale certificate, and may be used only to buy communications services for resale.
  • It expires every December 31.
  • Dealers who file electronically print their own; paper filers are mailed one in mid-November. Every active dealer can print or save it online.

Three ways to document a resale

The selling dealer must document every exempt sale for resale, using one of these: [GT-800011] [TIP 07A19-05] [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.]

Documenting a sale for resale
MethodHowValid for
Copy of the buyer's Annual Resale CertificateKeep a copy on file.Sales to that buyer during the calendar year. For a buyer who purchases on account on a continual basis, one certificate valid when received is enough; a new copy is not needed each year.
Transaction Resale Authorization NumberVerify the buyer's certificate number online (up to five numbers at once) or by calling 877-357-3725 and choosing communications services tax.That one transaction only.
Vendor Resale Authorization NumberThrough the online verification system; dealers can upload a batch file of up to 50,000 accounts and retrieve the numbers 24 hours later.Sales to that buyer during the calendar year.

When documentation is missing

A dealer that makes a sale for resale without complying with these requirements is liable for the tax on it. [s. 202.16, F.S.] [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.] The dealer must keep its resale documentation for as long as the Department can assess tax on the sale. [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.] [s. 202.16, F.S.]

Gulf Coast Connect sells to a reseller

Fictional businesses. Gulf Coast Connect sells wholesale lines every month to Mangrove Mobile, a registered CST dealer that bundles them into service for its own customers. [GT-800011] [TIP 07A19-05] [Rule 12A-19.060, F.A.C.]

  1. Mangrove buys on account on a continual basis, so in March 2026 Gulf Coast gets one copy of Mangrove's 2026 CST Annual Resale Certificate. It does not need a new copy in 2027 for these continual purchases.
  2. In July, a one-time buyer, Sandbar Telecom, orders a circuit for resale but has no certificate copy. Gulf Coast enters Sandbar's certificate number online and writes the Transaction Resale Authorization Number on the invoice. It is good for that sale only.
  3. For its 3,000 wholesale accounts, Gulf Coast uploads one batch file and retrieves Vendor Resale Authorization Numbers the next day, good for the calendar year.
  4. A buyer that gives a sales tax resale certificate instead of a CST one has not given valid CST documentation. Gulf Coast should get proper CST documentation or charge tax.

Key takeaways

  • Resale includes reselling, using as a component part, or integrating into a retail communications service.
  • The CST resale certificate (DR-700015) is separate from the sales tax one and expires December 31.
  • Document with a certificate copy, a Transaction Resale Authorization Number, or a Vendor Resale Authorization Number.
  • No proper documentation: the seller owes the tax.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. When does the CST Annual Resale Certificate expire?
Show the answer

Answer: December 31. The CST Annual Resale Certificate (DR-700015) expires every December 31, and each active registered dealer gets a new one each calendar year.

2. A Transaction Resale Authorization Number is valid for:
Show the answer

Answer: That one transaction. A Transaction Resale Authorization Number covers only the one transaction it was issued for. For ongoing purchases, use the buyer's Annual Resale Certificate instead.

3. May a buyer use its sales and use tax resale certificate to buy communications services tax-free for resale?
Show the answer

Answer: No, the CST certificate is separate. The CST Annual Resale Certificate is separate from the sales tax certificate.

4. A dealer sells for resale without proper documentation. Who owes the tax?
Show the answer

Answer: The selling dealer. A sale for resale is excluded only in strict compliance; otherwise the seller is liable.
