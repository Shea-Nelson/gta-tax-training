Module 15: Local rate changes and effective dates

Level: Advanced · About 20 minutes · Module 15 of 17

Learning objectives

After this module you will be able to:

  • Give the effective date and notice deadlines for a normal local rate change.
  • Explain how surtax changes and court rulings change local rates.
  • Apply the freeze on local rate increases through December 31, 2030.
  • Keep a billing system current with the Department's notices.

The normal schedule

Any adoption, repeal or change of a local CST rate takes effect for services on bills dated on or after the January 1 after the change. The city or county must notify the Department by September 1 before that January 1, on the Department's form, which is DR-700021, giving the rate, the effective date and a contact person. The Department must notify affected dealers at least 90 days before the effective date. [s. 202.21, F.S.] [Rule 12A-19.050, F.A.C.] [DR-700021]

The bill date controls, not the service date. The Department's January 2026 notice said its new rates applied “for all bills issued on or after January 1, 2026, regardless of the date the service is rendered.” [TIP 25A19-02] A local government that adjusts its rate by emergency ordinance under s. 202.20(2) must notify the Department immediately, and the Department must notify affected dealers within 30 days. [s. 202.21, F.S.]

The freeze through 2030

A local CST rate in effect on January 1, 2023 may not be increased before January 1, 2031, and any increase to a county discretionary sales surtax on or after January 1, 2023 may not be added to the local CST before then. [s. 202.19, F.S.] [TIP 25A19-01] The 2025 Legislature extended a freeze originally announced in TIP 23A19-01, which had been set to end December 31, 2025. [TIP 25A19-01] [TIP 23A19-01] Rates can still go down: the January 2026 changes were all decreases. [TIP 25A19-02]

Changes driven by the county surtax

Because the total local rate includes the county surtax conversion rate (Module 3), a change in a county's surtax changes the CST rate of every jurisdiction in that county. [TIP 25A19-02] [s. 202.20, F.S.]

Recent changes

  • January 1, 2026, Martin and Palm Beach counties: rates in jurisdictions in both counties changed because of a change in the county surtax. Stuart, for example, went to 5.220%. [TIP 25A19-02] [CST rate workbook]
  • March 2021, Hillsborough County: after the Florida Supreme Court struck down the county's 1% transportation surtax, the Department revised the local CST rates effective immediately, for example Tampa 6.020% and Plant City 6.520%. [TIP 21A19-01]
  • January 1, 2023: the City of Lake Worth was renamed Lake Worth Beach on the return with no rate change, and Bonita Springs changed its rate's expiration date. [TIP 22A19-02]

Jurisdictions added and removed

Jurisdictions also come and go: TIPs have announced new taxing jurisdictions and removed others, such as the Town of Hastings and the City of Weeki Wachee. Dealers must add and remove them in their systems on the dates given. [TIP 18A19-01] [TIP 18A19-02] [TIP 20A19-01]

Address changes have their own calendar

Changes to which addresses belong to which jurisdiction are separate from rate changes. They take effect only on January 1 or July 1, after local governments submit them at least 120 days ahead (Module 8). [DR-700002]

Staying current: a checklist

  1. Subscribe to the Department's TIP email alerts and read every TIP numbered with “A19,” the communications services tax series. [GT-800011] [TIPs page]
  2. Each fall, download the current “Historical, Current and Upcoming Local Rates” workbook and look for upcoming rates in red bold. [CST rate workbook] [GT-800011]
  3. Each January and July, download the updated address database. [GT-800011] [Address file downloads]
  4. Bill using the rate in effect on the bill date.

Rates for January 1, 2027

When this guide's sources were collected in November 2026, the Department's TIP index had no 2026 communications services tax TIPs, and the rate workbook (dated January 2026) showed no upcoming rates. Both were unchanged when checked again on November 22, 2026. Any January 1, 2027 changes would normally be announced in late November or December. Check the TIP index and rate table for them. [TIPs page] [CST rate workbook]

Key takeaways

  • Normal changes: local notice by September 1, Department notice at least 90 days ahead, effective for bills dated on or after January 1.
  • No increases before January 1, 2031; decreases are allowed.
  • Surtax changes and court rulings can change CST rates.
  • Address assignments change only January 1 and July 1.

Self-check

Choose an answer for each question, then select Check answer. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

1. A city adopts a new local CST rate in June. When is it effective (outside the freeze)?
Show the answer

Answer: Bills dated on or after the next January 1. Changes apply to bills dated on or after the January 1 after adoption.

2. By when must the city notify the Department?
Show the answer

Answer: September 1. Notice is due by September 1 before the January 1 effective date.

3. How much notice must the Department give dealers?
Show the answer

Answer: At least 90 days. Section 202.21 requires the Department to notify affected dealers at least 90 days before a local rate change takes effect. Normal changes then apply to bills dated on or after January 1.

4. Service was provided in December 2025 but billed on January 5, 2026. Which rate applies under the January 2026 TIP?
Show the answer

Answer: The new rate, because the bill was issued on or after January 1, 2026. The rates apply to all bills issued on or after January 1, 2026, regardless of when service was rendered.
